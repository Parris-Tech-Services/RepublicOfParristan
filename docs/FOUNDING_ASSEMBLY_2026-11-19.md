# Minutes of the Founding Assembly of the Republic of Parristan

**Date:** Thursday, 19 November 2026  
**Time:** 3:30 PM (AEDT)  
**Attendees:** Joshua Parris, Glenn, and three additional founding members.  
**Total attendance:** 5/5 founders; 100% quorum established.

**Provenance:** Minutes provided by founding organiser Joshua Parris on 19 November 2026. They record the founders' decisions as reported; a separately signed copy or independent ballot check has not been provided to this repository.

## 1. Opening of Assembly

The assembly was called to order at 3:30 PM. It was confirmed that all five founding members were present and had given consent to proceed with the founding vote.

## 2. Amendment to Assembly Notice Rules

- **Motion:** To amend the drafted constitutional rules regarding assembly notice, changing the required notice period from 14 days to 1 hour.
- **Vote:** 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN.
- **Resolution:** Motion passed unanimously. The 1-hour notice rule is formally adopted according to the supplied minutes.

## 3. Ratification of the Constitution

- **Motion:** To formally adopt the Constitution of the Republic of Parristan.
- **Vote:** 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN.
- **Resolution:** Motion passed unanimously. The founders record the Constitution as ratified and formally in force.
- **Effective date:** Thursday, 19 November 2026.

## 4. Strategic Direction of National Systems

- **Motion:** To officially model Parristan's administrative structure and sovereign IT systems after the frameworks used by the French National Gendarmerie (GendBuntu) and the German state of Schleswig-Holstein.
- **Resolution:** Motion passed unanimously. The assembly adopted an open-source methodology centred on Linux workstations, LibreOffice and open formats.

## 5. Official Appointments

- **Motion:** To appoint Glenn to the role of Chief Digital Officer.
- **Resolution:** Passed by unanimous consent. Glenn accepted the appointment and assumed leadership of the Office of Sovereign Systems (OSPO). The minutes record the OSPO's proposed charter in the Open Source Act as active.

## 6. Closing

The founding assembly having achieved its agenda was adjourned. Elections and the establishment of functioning institutions will proceed under the newly ratified rules.

## Editorial record and outstanding primary evidence

The six sections above reflect the actual content provided by the founding organiser. The following are **documentary limitations**, not additional resolutions:

1. **Exact constitutional text:** The minutes refer to the Constitution but not a definitive document version/commit SHA. The text voted upon should be archived before marking a particular repository draft as the enacted text.
2. **Notice:** The minutes say the proposed notice rule was reduced from 14 days to one hour by unanimous vote, but do not state when actual notice was sent or the prior authority for amending notice at that meeting. That procedural question has not been independently assessed.
3. **Open Source Act:** The strategic direction and OSPO charter were approved, but these minutes do not expressly identify a separately enacted version of *every clause* of the Open Source Act draft.
4. **Elections:** Glenn's appointment is administrative, not an elected Presidency; no presidential election result is recorded.
5. **Attendees:** The supplied minutes name Joshua and Glenn and describe the remaining three as founding members, without attributing individual ballots; the separate [founders register](FOUNDERS.md) holds the five names.
6. **Legal position:** These are internal decisions of a micronational association, not Australian public law or evidence of internationally recognised statehood.
