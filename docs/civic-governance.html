Real-world association

Real human members participate voluntarily under Parristan's Constitution and applicable Australian law. Real expenses, membership, consent and contracts remain separate from the fictional game. No rights arise over NSW land merely from a map.

Fictional world

The game can include AI residents, local civic associations, postal counters, community halls, simulated police-shaped Civic Service agents and virtual rates paid in fictional currency. None creates a real citizen, council, debt, land tax, government office or criminal power.
