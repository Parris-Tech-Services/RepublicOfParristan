## Minutes of the Founding Assembly of the Republic of Parristan

**Date:** Thursday, 19 November 2026
**Time:** 3:30 PM (AEDT)
**Attendees:** Joshua Parris, Glenn, and three additional founding members. (Total attendance: 5/5 founders. 100% quorum established.)

### 1. Opening of Assembly
The assembly was called to order at 3:30 PM. It was confirmed that all five founding members were present and had given consent to proceed with the founding vote.

### 2. Amendment to Assembly Notice Rules
- **Motion:** To amend the drafted constitutional rules regarding assembly notice, changing the required notice period from 14 days to 1 hour.
- **Vote:** 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN.
- **Resolution:** Motion passed unanimously. The 1-hour notice rule is formally adopted.

### 3. Ratification of the Constitution
- **Motion:** To formally adopt the Constitution of the Republic of Parristan.
- **Vote:** 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN.
- **Resolution:** Motion passed unanimously. The Constitution is ratified and formally in force.
- **Effective Date:** Thursday, 19 November 2026.

### 4. Strategic Direction of National Systems
- **Motion:** To officially model the Republic of Parristan's administrative structure and sovereign IT systems after the frameworks utilized by the French National Gendarmerie (GendBuntu) and the German state of Schleswig-Holstein.
- **Resolution:** Motion passed unanimously. Parristan's infrastructure will adhere strictly to an open-source methodology, leveraging Linux workstations, LibreOffice, and open formats to ensure complete digital sovereignty.

### 5. Official Appointments
- **Motion:** To officially appoint Glenn to the role of Chief Digital Officer.
- **Resolution:** Motion passed by unanimous consent. Glenn accepts the appointment and assumes immediate leadership of the Office of Sovereign Systems (OSPO). The OSPO's proposed charter in the Open Source Act is now active.

### 6. Closing
The founding assembly having achieved its agenda, the meeting was adjourned. Elections and the establishment of functioning institutions will proceed under the newly ratified rules.
