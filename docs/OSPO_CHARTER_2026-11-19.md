# Office of Sovereign Systems (OSPO) — Activated Charter Record

**Reported decision:** Founding Assembly of 19 November 2026, commencing 3:30 pm AEDT  
**Status:** Founding members unanimously activated the proposed OSPO charter and appointed Glenn as Chief Digital Officer (CDO); accepted by Glenn, per supplied minutes.  
**Scope:** Internal administration of the voluntary Republic of Parristan association, not sovereign powers.

## Appointment and accountability

- **Chief Digital Officer:** Glenn (identified as Glenn Peterson in the existing [founders register](FOUNDERS.md)).
- **Appointing authority for this initial office:** Unanimous founding-assembly resolution, not a presidential appointment.
- **President:** Not yet elected or recorded.
- **Mandate:** Lead Parristan's open-source-first digital administration and report to the Assembly.
- **Charter basis:** [Open Source Act / OSPO charter proposal](OPEN_SOURCE_ACT_DRAFT.md), Part 2, activated by founding resolution specifically for the OSPO. The entire separately proposed *Open Source Act 2026* has **not** been recorded as enacted.
- **Future appointment procedure:** The alternative [bill](../operational-pack/government/open-source-act.md) proposes that the President appoint the CDO. That future rule needs harmonisation with this initial Assembly appointment when the bill is formally considered; it cannot be retroactively represented as a presidential action.

## Operating duties

1. Maintain an inventory of official services, operators, software licences, hosting, data location and recovery methods.
2. Run staged pilots of Linux and LibreOffice, followed as justified by collaboration, directory and communications tools.
3. Record open-source exemptions, reasons and expiry dates; submit any binding policy/expenditure requirements for approval under adopted association procedures.
4. Establish secure access, encryption, patching, open data exports and regular verified backups/restores for services that are actually deployed.
5. Publish honest progress and incident reports to the Assembly, omitting private membership, identity, financial and voting data.
6. Identify a second authorised administrator before any critical service depends on one person.

## Immediate scope and limits

- The office is organisationally **active by the reported resolution**; no infrastructure has been verified deployed by the appointment alone.
- No unauthorised access to third-party, employer, school or client infrastructure.
- No land jurisdiction, public-law authority, statutory enforcement, spending power or government recognition arises from the appointment.
- Actual hosting, budgets, operator accounts and procurement decisions remain to be separately authorised and documented.
