Rules currently in force

Written motions

At least three eligible member votes. Simple majority of valid YES/NO votes. 48 hours, or close early if all five vote.

Constitutional amendments

Full text and at least fourteen days' notice, two-thirds of valid votes and quorum. The founding one-hour notice amendment does not apply.

Presidential and other elections

Independent Returning Officer and certified nominations. A sole candidate requires YES/NO confirmation; contested ballots require a majority or a new runoff.

Secret election voting in this portal is locked until the new rules, ballot custody and independent security review are accepted. A five-member ballot cannot guarantee perfect anonymity.
