## Interpretation

The founders unanimously passed a procedural motion before the ratification motion, with all members present and consenting. The minutes expressly declare ratification and an effective date; **this register reflects that decision as a founder-supplied record**. Independent verification of the originals and precise notice timing is still outstanding.

The one-hour founding notice resolution **does not change Article XI** of the constitution. Future amendments remain governed by its published 14-day notice and two-thirds-majority rule unless that article is itself properly amended.

Ratification concerns **Parristan's internal association governance**, not recognition as an independent country under Australian or international law.
