# Founding Execution Checklist — Republic of Parristan

**Working checklist · 19 November 2026 · Status: organising plan, not an adopted law.**

This checklist translates the existing [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) into tasks that the five founding participants can actually complete. Mark an item done **only with a dated evidence link or private verification record**. Meetings, decisions, infrastructure and formal offices must not be represented as completed before they occur.

## Already established or prepared

- [x] Republic of Parristan name and founding initiative established.
- [x] Separate internal Enterprise repository and public Gazette repository exist.
- [x] Five founding members identified and, according to the organiser, agreed to participate. Formal meeting/signature evidence is still to be recorded.
- [x] Draft constitution, founding declaration, assembly procedures, member policy and roadmaps published.
- [x] Public Gazette website source prepared. **Live hosting is not verified by this checklist.**
- [x] Open-source digital-government strategy **proposed**, modelled on GendBuntu and Schleswig-Holstein; **not ratified or implemented**.

## Priority 1 — move from intention to a functioning association

### Before the first founding assembly

- [ ] **Confirm and record the five founders' participation**, their permission for any public naming, and a secure contact route (do not store private contact details in public GitHub).
- [ ] Agree a one-paragraph mission, public-service purpose and first-year goals.
- [ ] Agree the founding assembly's eligibility, notice, quorum and voting procedure **before** the vote. Use the existing ratification proposal as a starting point.
- [ ] Choose a meeting date and accessible venue or online option; send formal notice and full draft text, respecting the proposed 14-day notice period if adopted.
- [ ] Prepare agenda, chair/secretary for the meeting, attendance verification and private voting method. Temporary roles are not elected government offices.
- [ ] Collect constitutional amendments and settle the text members will vote on.

**Evidence gate:** notice, agenda, approved procedure and eligible voter count exist; no invented signatures or votes.

### At the founding assembly

- [ ] Confirm quorum and formally record attendance and conflicts.
- [ ] Debate and vote on the constitution using the agreed procedure.
- [ ] Record the exact text, YES/NO/ABSTAIN totals, outcome and date in signed-off minutes.
- [ ] **If ratified**, publish an adopted version and decision entry; **if not**, keep it a draft and schedule further consultation.
- [ ] Approve interim operational roles and election timetable only within the authority actually granted by the adopted rules.

**Evidence gate:** ratification status can be checked against genuine minutes; a repository merge alone is not a vote.

## Priority 2 — first 30 days after valid ratification

- [ ] Schedule and conduct the first democratic election under adopted rules; record results, terms and the transfer of responsibilities.
- [ ] Set up a private, opt-in membership process, privacy notice, retention/deletion rules and an appeal route.
- [ ] Keep a public decision register and regular meeting minutes with private details redacted.
- [ ] Adopt a small annual budget, spending approval limits, a financial ledger and dual review of expenditure.
- [ ] Evaluate an appropriate Australian legal structure. A **NSW incorporated association** is one possible route, not sovereignty; check the constitution, 5-member minimum, committee/public-officer rules and registration requirements before applying.
- [ ] Confirm who owns the domain, public site, account recovery and administrative access, with a second authorised backup administrator.

**Evidence gate:** members know how decisions are made; money, access and membership are handled with real controls.

## Priority 3 — build a useful digital administration

- [ ] Run a **one-workstation/VM Linux + LibreOffice** pilot and publish a reproducible setup guide.
- [ ] Establish an official document template using open formats and accessible PDF export.
- [ ] Pilot standards-based email/calendar access and role accounts; do not claim a government mail service before it works.
- [ ] Try privacy-aware file sharing (for example Nextcloud) and a low-friction meeting tool; test access removal.
- [ ] Inventory third-party dependencies and back up founding documents and Gazette content.
- [ ] Run one documented encrypted backup **and restore test**, with a second operator able to recover the data.
- [ ] Add a minimal documentation/link-check workflow that does not waste CI runs; confirm the public site actually deploys before announcing a live address.

**Evidence gate:** basic office, communications and records work end-to-end; every claimed service has a verified URL/test result. See [Open-Source Digital Government Strategy](OPEN_SOURCE_DIGITAL_GOVERNMENT.md).

## Priority 4 — first 90 days and beyond

- [ ] Choose and adopt original national symbols through an actual recorded procedure.
- [ ] Offer one modest, measurable civic or educational project for members and the public.
- [ ] Establish a lawful meeting venue or correspondence address, with the property owner's permission.
- [ ] Create a public annual-style status page showing actual participation, services, finances and decisions.
- [ ] Adopt a diplomatic correspondence process; engage other micronations truthfully without claiming official state recognition.
- [ ] Research property/territorial questions separately with appropriate legal advice; do not claim sovereignty over land without evidence.
- [ ] Review the digital pilot against cost, supportability, accessibility, security and ability to move hosts.
- [ ] Review whether a sustainable registered association, larger membership or service needs justify more complex infrastructure.

## Four separate kinds of progress

| Track | What counts as progress | What does **not** count |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Civic legitimacy | Consenting members, transparent votes, functioning internal institutions | Naming a President without a lawful election |
| Legal organisation | Accurate Australian legal structure, contracts and compliance | Declaring exemption from Australian law |
| Digital independence | Real working open-source services, tested exports/restores and support | Merely listing Linux/Nextcloud in a plan |
| International status | Independently documented external contacts; clear evidence for any claim | Calling correspondence diplomatic recognition |

**Important:** Parristan remains an aspiring micronational community, **not a recognised sovereign state**. Completing the checklist does not make it a sovereign country or establish recognised citizenship, passports, jurisdiction or diplomatic immunity.

Useful NSW references: [About incorporated associations](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/incorporated-associations/about-incorporated-associations), [registration requirements](https://www.service.nsw.gov.au/transaction/apply-to-register-an-incorporated-association), and [constitution requirements](https://www.nsw.gov.au/business-and-economy/incorporated-associations/starting-an-incorporated-association/an-incorporated-associations-constitution).

## Project-management rule

For each unchecked item, track **owner, target date, current status, evidence, blockers and next action**. Keep names and sensitive materials only in access-restricted systems; public GitHub issues should reference the task, not the private evidence.
