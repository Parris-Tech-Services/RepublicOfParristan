Gazette notice 2026-004 · 19 November 2026

First founding assembly held

Organiser's report: five founders present, constitution approved 5–0.

Notice published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Organiser-reported; minutes not yet transcribed. Authority: Founding initiative organiser. Legal effect: None under Australian public law.

What is reported

The founding organiser reports that all five founding members attended the first founding assembly at 3:30 pm AEDT on Thursday 19 November 2026.

Update, 3:44 pm AEDT

The organiser further reports that all five founders voted YES (5–0), that the constitution takes effect on 19 November 2026, and that the notice rule is to be changed to 1 hour.

What is not yet verified

The exact text voted on, the wording and timing of the notice rule, and reviewed minutes are still being recorded. This is an organiser's report, not an independently verified result.
