Gazette notice 2026-002 · 19 November 2026

Proposal for the first constituent assembly

Proposed steps towards a genuine, properly recorded constituent assembly.

Notice published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Meeting not yet scheduled. Authority: Founding initiative organisers. Legal effect: None under Australian public law.

Purpose

The founding initiative proposes a meeting of real, consenting participants to review the declaration and draft constitution and agree on fair, published rules for constitutional adoption.

Next milestone

The proposed procedure calls for an announced electorate, advance circulation of a fixed constitution, appropriate notice, a genuine quorum and verifiable vote totals. These rules themselves must be agreed before the meeting.

Current position

No assembly date has been announced, no attendees enrolled for a real ballot and no constitutional result recorded. This publication is a planning notice, not a record of an assembly that has already occurred.
