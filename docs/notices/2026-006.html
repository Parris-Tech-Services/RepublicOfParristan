Gazette notice 2026-006 · 19 November 2026

Interim official channel adopted

The founders voted 5–0 to make their encrypted Matrix room Parristan's interim official channel.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Adopted (5–0), written resolution. Legal effect: Internal voluntary association decision only, not Australian public law.

Motion 1 — Interim official channel

Until permanent systems are adopted, the founders' encrypted Matrix/Element room is the Republic of Parristan's official channel for notices, discussion and votes. Glenn (CDO) is asked to propose permanent email and chat arrangements within 14 days.

All five founders joined the room and voted 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN. Voting closed at 4:34 pm AEDT on 19 November 2026.

Next step

The CDO's proposal for permanent email and chat is due by 3 December 2026. The room is private; its address and member usernames are not published.
