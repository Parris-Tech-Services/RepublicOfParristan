Gazette notice 2026-005 · 19 November 2026

Ratification and digital appointments

The constitution ratified 5–0 and Glenn appointed Chief Digital Officer.

Notice published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Organiser-supplied minutes. Legal effect: None under Australian public law.

Decisions recorded

The assembly changed the founding-assembly notice from 14 days to 1 hour (5–0–0), ratified the constitution (5–0–0, effective 19 November 2026), adopted an open-source-first strategy modelled on GendBuntu and Schleswig-Holstein, and appointed Glenn as Chief Digital Officer.

Notice 2026-006 later corrected and supplemented this record.
