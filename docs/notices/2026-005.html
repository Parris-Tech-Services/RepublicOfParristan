Gazette notice 2026-005 · 19 November 2026

Unanimous constitutional ratification and digital appointment

The five-member founding assembly recorded a 5–0 constitutional vote, an open-source strategy and Glenn's Chief Digital Officer appointment.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Ratified (5–0), organiser minutes. Legal effect: Internal voluntary association decisions only, not Australian public law.

Constitution ratified

Five founders attended. The Assembly voted 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN to replace the proposed founding notice period of 14 days with 1 hour; it then voted 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN to adopt the Constitution, with internal effect from 19 November 2026.

The one-hour rule concerns the founding meeting; the separate constitutional amendment rule of 14 days remains in the published Constitution. The exact meeting copy and actual notice-delivery time have not been supplied for corroboration.

Digital administration and CDO

Members unanimously endorsed a Linux and open-format strategy inspired by GendBuntu and Schleswig-Holstein. Glenn was unanimously appointed and accepted as the head of the Office of Sovereign Systems. No particular IT service is thereby verified as deployed; the entire separate Open Source Act bill has not been voted through as such.

Next electoral milestone

No President has yet been elected. The consolidated Constitution anticipates an election within 90 days of ratification, by 17 February 2027 on the reported effective date.
