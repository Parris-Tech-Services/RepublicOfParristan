Gazette 2026-016 · 23 November 2026

Civic governance and Repository Town integration

President-reported unanimous vote: 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN. Ordinary policy only; certification pending.

Published 23 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Ordinary resolution PA-2026-001: The President reports all five eligible members voted YES. Secretary verification of original ballots and procedure is outstanding. No constitutional amendment is enacted by this record.

Immediate scope of the motion

Parristan adopts internal policy distinguishing human membership from fictional AI residents, authorising fictional Local Civic Associations (beginning in Tealford), simulation-only Civic Service, virtual money and rates, and staged Repository Town / Lantern Point / Tealford game integration. Real NSW government, property, police, planning, tax and emergency-service powers are unaffected.

Implementation policy: full operative civic and simulation policy.

Complete approved motion: Gazette 2026-016 Markdown source.

Separate constitutional proposal

The Assembly authorised publication and consultation of Article XIII. It did not ratify Article XIII. The full proposal appears in Gazette 2026-017 and requires its own notice period and vote under Article XI.

Verification pending

Secretary certification of the voter roll, exact text, written-vote timing, quorum and any meeting notice is pending. Neither a GitHub commit nor a report by the President substitutes for this verification. See the public decision register.
