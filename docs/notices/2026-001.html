Gazette notice 2026-001 · 19 November 2026

Founding initiative and constitutional consultation

An invitation to review the founding documents of a developing micronation.

Notice published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Published — consultation open. Authority: Founding initiative organisers. Legal effect: None under Australian public law.

Public notice

The Republic of Parristan founding initiative has published a proposed declaration, draft constitution and state-building roadmap for public consideration. It seeks to form a voluntary, democratic micronational community through real participation and accountable rules.

Status and legal effect

The proposed constitution has not been ratified. There is no elected President, recognised sovereign territory or state-level diplomatic recognition. This notice has no statutory legal effect under Australian law.

Consultation

Public comments on rights, democratic decision-making, assembly rules and constitutional safeguards are invited through the linked GitHub issue. A public comment does not count as a constitutional vote or citizenship application.
