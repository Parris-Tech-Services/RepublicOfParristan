Gazette notice 2026-011 · 19 November 2026

Official email addresses in service

Office mailboxes on parristan.org are set up and tested.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: In service, tested 19 November 2026. Authority: Motion 5 (Gazette 2026-010). Legal effect: Internal voluntary association notice only.

Official addresses

AddressOffice
admin@parristan.orgAccount administration (joint admins)
assembly@parristan.orgWhole Assembly (delivers to all office mailboxes)
cdo@parristan.orgChief Digital Officer
registrar@parristan.orgRegistrar
secretary@parristan.orgSecretary
treasurer@parristan.orgTreasurer
returningofficer@parristan.orgReturning Officer
president@parristan.orgPresident (reserved until the first election result)

These are office mailboxes: they pass to the next officeholder.

Setup

  • Domain parristan.org registered 19 Nov 2026 (Cloudflare Registrar), held on behalf of the association; renews annually.
  • Email by Purelymail, with SPF, DKIM and DMARC protection against forged mail.
  • Tests passed: sending out, receiving replies, and group delivery to assembly@.
  • Cost: about US$20 a year in total, to be recorded by the Treasurer.

The Matrix room remains the official channel for votes and debate.
