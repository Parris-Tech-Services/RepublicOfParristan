Gazette notice 2026-003 · 19 November 2026

National symbols released for consultation

The original Parristan flag and coat of arms are available for public comment.

Notice published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Design proposals only. Authority: Founding initiative organisers. Legal effect: None under Australian public law.

The Founding Star flag

A navy and teal flag with an ivory divider and an eight-point golden star is proposed as a distinctive visual identity for the future republic.

Civic arms and anthem

A matching shield and a proposed anthem lyric, Together Stand, are available for discussion. These are original proposals, not official emblems or a state anthem.

Adoption process

National symbols will remain proposals until an authorised, properly recorded decision adopts them. The emblem is not a government seal recognised by the Australian state.
