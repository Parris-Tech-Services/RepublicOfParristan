Gazette notice 2026-007 · 19 November 2026

Returning Officer appointed; election timetable

Matthew Walker appointed Returning Officer 5–0; first presidential election timetable announced.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Adopted (5–0), written resolution. Legal effect: Internal voluntary association decision only.

Motion 2 — Returning Officer

The founders appointed Matthew Walker as Returning Officer for the first election, 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN (Joshua Parris, the mover and a candidate, voted last; corrected from an initial 4–0 report). Matthew accepted and will not be a candidate.

First presidential election

Joshua Parris has declared he will stand. Nominations: 20–27 November 2026. Voting: 28 November – 1 December 2026. With a single candidate, a YES/NO confirmation vote applies. No President has been elected yet.
