Gazette notice 2026-014 · 19 November 2026

The President's first programme

Motion 7 adopted en bloc, reported unanimous: goals, money rules, membership, website and symbols timetable.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Adopted — reported 5–0, en bloc. Legal effect: Internal voluntary association decision only.

Motion 7, moved by President Joshua Parris

  1. Mission statement and five first-year goals adopted; President reports progress monthly.
  2. President chairs Assembly meetings; Secretary takes minutes.
  3. Votes and debate in the Matrix room; notices and external correspondence by official email.
  4. First monthly meeting in the week of 7 December 2026, date set by the Secretary by poll.
  5. US$18.50 founding costs (domain and email, 19 Nov) recorded as a donation, not reimbursed.
  6. Financial controls adopted: $50 Treasurer; $50–$250 Treasurer plus one officer; above that by Assembly vote. No bank account until the Assembly decides one is needed.
  7. Membership policy, privacy notice and appeals adopted; under-18s may join as non-voting junior members with guardian consent.
  8. Official website moved to parristan.org.
  9. Flag, coat of arms and motto to be voted on separately in January 2027; anthem melody ideas collected.
  10. Membership applications open to invited people from about March 2027.
