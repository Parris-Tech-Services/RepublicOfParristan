Gazette notice 2026-012 · 19 November 2026

First presidential election brought forward

Founders voted 5–0 to hold the first presidential election on 19 November 2026.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Adopted 5–0, closed 5:51 pm AEDT. Legal effect: Internal voluntary association decision only.

Motion 6

The Motion 2 timetable (Gazette 2026-007) is replaced: nominations open and close on 19 November 2026; with one valid candidate, a YES/NO confirmation vote follows the same day, by confidential email ballot to the Returning Officer. All members agreed to this method.

Result: 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN.

Votes are not secret from the Returning Officer; only totals are published.
