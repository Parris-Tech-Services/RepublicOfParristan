Gazette notice 2026-009 · 19 November 2026

Standing procedures adopted; constitution text confirmed

Voting, meeting, records and admin procedures adopted 5–0; Constitution v0.1 confirmed as the adopted text.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Adopted (5–0), en bloc written resolution. Legal effect: Internal voluntary association decision only.

Motion 4 (en bloc): How we work

The founders voted 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN (closed 4:47 pm AEDT) to adopt:

  1. Voting: motions open 48 hours or until all have voted; majority of votes cast; at least 3 voting.
  2. Meetings: monthly, arranged by the Secretary; extra meetings on 24 hours' notice.
  3. Records: Secretary keeps minutes and decisions for the Gazette; no personal contact details published.
  4. Admin access: Josh and Glenn joint admins of the channel, GitHub organisation and any future domain.
  5. Constitution check: the published Constitution v0.1 is confirmed as the text adopted on 19 November 2026.

No item was split out.
