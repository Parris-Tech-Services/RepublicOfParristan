Gazette notice 2026-008 · 19 November 2026

Interim office-holders appointed

Secretary, Treasurer and Registrar appointed 5–0 in one en bloc motion.

Published 19 November 2026 · Australia/Sydney

Status: Adopted (5–0), en bloc written resolution. Legal effect: Internal voluntary association decision only.

Motion 3 (en bloc)

The founders voted 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN to:

  1. Appoint Jane Carbunkle (Secretary), Fred Carbunkle (Treasurer) and Glenn Peterson (Registrar) until the first election.
  2. Ask the President-elect to bring a draft mission and first-year goals within 14 days of the election.
  3. Keep the member list private (never on GitHub), with member numbers 1–5 issued to the founders.
  4. Hold no money and no bank account until a budget is approved.

No member asked for an item to be voted separately. Joshua Parris also posted his candidate statement for President; no one has been elected.
