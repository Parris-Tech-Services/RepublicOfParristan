Can I become a citizen?

Not yet. A private, consent-based application and membership process needs to be adopted and established first. A GitHub comment, star, email or issue does not constitute citizenship or an application.

When membership opens, it will be voluntary membership in a micronational association and will not be a nationality recognised by Australia.

Your privacy: Do not publish personal addresses, identity documents, dates of birth, phone numbers, medical details or private contact information on GitHub. No form on this site collects your data.

How are actual decisions made?

A proposal becomes an adopted constitutional decision only through the lawful, announced process agreed by the participants, with verifiable vote totals. A GitHub issue is a public conversation, not a ballot.
