# “Together Stand” — proposed Parristan anthem

**Original lyric proposal, 19 November 2026. Not an adopted national anthem.**

### Verse 1

Upon the dawn, our voices rise,  
Beneath the wide and open skies.  
With steady hands and hearts made free,  
We build in hope and dignity.

### Refrain

Parristan, together stand,  
In truth and peace we join our hands.  
Let justice guide the work we do,  
A common good, a promise true.

### Verse 2

No crown of pride, no boast of might,  
But care for all and love of right.  
A willing people, brave and kind,  
With open hearts and steadfast mind.

*Repeat the refrain.*

No copyrighted existing melody is implied or used. A musical setting and official adoption have not occurred.
