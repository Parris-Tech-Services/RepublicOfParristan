Corrections and contact

Report an error or contact the Republic

Open a public issue on GitHub. Please do not include addresses, phone numbers or other private details — issues are public. Founders review issues and reply there.

How corrections work

  1. Historical notices are never silently rewritten.
  2. A correction is recorded with its date, what changed and why.
  3. Material corrections get their own Gazette notice.
