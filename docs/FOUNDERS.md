## Why this register is preliminary

Being named by the organiser is not the same as signing the founding declaration, accepting citizenship or participating in a constitutional vote. The final register should be established by an actual opt-in process. No office or government authority is conferred by this list.

## Founding assembly: the next democratic step

1. Confirm each person's intention to participate using a private channel.
2. Obtain permission before publishing members' names beyond the existing internal repository.
3. Confirm each participant has seen the [draft constitution](CONSTITUTION_DRAFT.md) and [founding declaration](FOUNDING_DECLARATION.md).
4. Agree on the meeting date, participation method, voting eligibility and quorum.
5. Record attendance, proposed amendments and the actual vote on ratification.
6. Record any interim appointments or later elections separately and accurately.

A [founding assembly minutes template](FOUNDING_ASSEMBLY_MINUTES_TEMPLATE.md) is provided to help document this process.

## Information deliberately omitted

Home addresses, phone numbers, email addresses, birth dates, family and employment details, personal anecdotes and unverified claims about individuals are **not** stored in this repository. Such information is unnecessary for a public-facing founding-member list and could expose people if repository access expands.

This document is an internal administrative record for a developing micronation, not proof of recognised legal citizenship or statehood.
