# Proposed Founding Declaration of the Republic of Parristan

**Draft — not adopted.** Prepared 19 November 2026 for public consideration by prospective members.

We, the prospective founding citizens of the Republic of Parristan, affirm our intention to establish a peaceful, voluntary and democratically governed micronational community.

We declare the following aspirations:

1. To create a durable community with institutions governed by a written constitution.
2. To protect human dignity, conscience, equality before internal rules, privacy and freedom of association.
3. To organise democratic deliberation, fair elections and accountable stewardship of shared resources.
4. To build genuine cultural and civic life, including traditions, education, public projects and mutual service.
5. To pursue lawful and peaceful dialogue with other micronations and, where appropriate, established institutions.
6. To be truthful about Parristan's international position, jurisdiction, population and territorial circumstances.
7. To respect the laws and rights of all people and jurisdictions where our members reside.

We do **not** claim that this declaration alone establishes a sovereign state, creates diplomatic recognition, transfers property, or exempts any person from Australian or other applicable law.

## Adoption process

The declaration becomes the internal founding declaration **only** after adoption by the constitutional assembly under an announced process with a recorded vote. Until then it remains a proposal.
