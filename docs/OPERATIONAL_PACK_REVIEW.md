# Uploaded Operational Pack v1 — status review

**19 November 2026 — proposed creative/reference material; not enacted civic records.**

The supplied Parristan Operational Pack v1 ZIP contains proposed national symbols, traditions, the Parri currency, mock passports, government-role descriptions, citizenship application forms, a populated founder CSV and machine-readable game lore.

The existing public Gazette says the constitution is unratified, the government is unelected, citizenship intake is not live and no symbols have been adopted. The supplied sample CSV instead says citizenship was granted on 19 November, and the creative pack assigns offices including a President for Life. Importing those as official records would contradict the public status register.

The pack therefore remains **reviewed but not imported as official/operational records**. Its creative concepts are suitable for future clearly marked game/design proposals. Any public demo would use blank example data, with mock passports labelled non-valid and actual positions/elections represented only after independently recorded decisions. The ZIP itself has not been copied into the repo; the source remains the organiser-supplied attachment.

No person has been enrolled, elected, appointed or granted citizenship by this review.
