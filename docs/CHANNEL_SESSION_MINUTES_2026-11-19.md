The chair created the room and invited the four other founders. All five had joined by 4:31 pm. The chair made Glenn Peterson (CDO) a room admin.

**Carried 5–0–0** (closed 4:34 pm). CDO proposal due 3 December 2026.

Joshua Parris declared he would stand for President.

> Appoint Matthew Walker as Returning Officer for the first election. They won't be a candidate.

**Carried 5–0–0.** Matthew Walker accepted. Proposed timetable: nominations 20–27 November, voting 28 November – 1 December 2026; a single candidate faces a YES/NO confirmation vote.

Joshua Parris posted his candidate statement. Members responded positively. No election has yet taken place.

## 4. Motion 3 (en bloc) — Interim roles and first steps

1. Jane Carbunkle as Secretary, Fred Carbunkle as Treasurer and Glenn Peterson as Registrar until the first election.
2. The President-elect to bring a draft mission and first-year goals within 14 days of the election.
3. The Registrar keeps the member list privately, never on GitHub, and issues member numbers 1–5 to the founders.
4. Parristan holds no money and no bank account until a budget is approved.

No item was split. **Carried 5–0–0.**

## 5. Motion 4 (en bloc) — How we work

1. Written votes stay open 48 hours or until all have voted; majority of votes cast; at least 3 voting.
2. Monthly meetings arranged by the Secretary; extra meetings on 24 hours' notice.
3. Secretary keeps minutes and decisions for the Gazette; no personal contact details published.
4. Josh and Glenn joint admins of the room, GitHub organisation and any future domain.
5. Constitution v0.1 in the repository confirmed as the text adopted today.

No item was split. **Carried 5–0–0** (closed 4:47 pm).
