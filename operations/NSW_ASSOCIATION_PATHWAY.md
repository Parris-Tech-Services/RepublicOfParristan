# Optional NSW Incorporated Association Pathway

**Research note prepared 19 November 2026.** An incorporated association could provide a lawful Australian organisational vehicle for Parristan's member meetings, assets and administration. **It would not make Parristan an internationally sovereign country.**

## Verified NSW basics (current government guidance)

- NSW Government states incorporated associations generally require **at least five members**.
- Service NSW states a committee requires at least **three adult members**, with at least three committee members residing in Australia (the model constitution may prescribe a larger committee). Exact eligibility and constitution wording must be checked before lodging.
- The association needs an appropriate constitution compliant with the **Associations Incorporation Act 2009 (NSW)** and requirements including its Schedule 1.
- Incorporation is optional for a voluntary group, but can offer a distinct legal structure; there are ongoing governance, records and financial reporting obligations.
- The existing micronational constitution has different purposes from an Australian association's statutory constitution and should not be submitted as compliant without a clause-by-clause review.
