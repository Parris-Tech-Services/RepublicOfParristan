# National Symbols — Proposals and Adoption Ballot

**Status:** original symbol proposals are ready; there has been **no recorded national adoption vote**.

## Existing proposals

1. **Flag A: Founding Star** — original navy, teal, ivory and gold eight-point-star design, in [editable SVG](../docs/assets/flag-proposal.svg).
2. **Civic arms A: Liberty · Service · Truth** — original corresponding shield and branches, in [editable SVG](../docs/assets/arms-proposal.svg).
3. **Anthem lyric A: Together Stand** — original lyrics, in the [anthem proposal](../docs/ANTHEM_PROPOSAL.md); no adopted melody or recording.
4. **Motto options:** *Liberty · Service · Truth* (the arms proposal), *Ex Codice Libertas* (older exploratory wording), or another proposal. The founders must choose; both are currently suggestions.

## Practical adoption process

1. Confirm the Assembly has constitutional authority to adopt official symbols and whether quorum/majority rules apply.
2. Circulate all current proposals and allow additional designs and critiques. Check for third-party trademarks and copyright conflicts.
3. Freeze final specifications and ensure the Republic has permission to publish, reproduce and adapt the chosen artwork and any anthem recording.
4. Conduct **separate votes** for flag, arms, motto, anthem text and later musical setting.
5. Publish actual date, vote totals and adopted SVG/version/source only after the vote has happened. Until then every symbol remains labelled **proposed**.
6. Define approved use (website, stationery, seals, ceremonies) without pretending official Australian or international government authority.
