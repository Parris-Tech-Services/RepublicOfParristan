**Prepared:** 19 November 2026. **Status:** HISTORICAL PREPARATION PACK. The organiser now reports a five-person assembly at 3:30 pm AEDT, 19 November 2026. See the [event record](../docs/FOUNDING_ASSEMBLY_2026-11-19.md); exact vote counts and notice particulars remain unrecorded.

## The five founders

Joshua Parris; Glenn Peterson; Matthew John Lucas Walker; Fred Carbunkle; Jane Carbunkle. The organising founder reports all five have agreed to establish Parristan. Their first collective assembly was subsequently reported held; the original motion counts must still be transcribed.

## Preparation

1. Circulate the complete [draft constitution](../docs/CONSTITUTION_DRAFT.md) as a pinned commit SHA and a copy each founder can read.
2. Obtain agreement on the assembly rules **before any ballot** (including eligibility, quorum, majority, chair, teller and remote participation). The existing proposed rules are three members present (including two other than the chair), 14 full days' notice after circulation of a fixed text, and at least two-thirds YES among YES+NO votes.
3. Issue a dated notice: meeting date and time (AEST/AEDT stated), place or video link via private channel, agenda, fixed constitution SHA, proposed rule text, nomination method and contact method for questions.
4. Appoint a neutral teller if practicable. Record actual attendance, total eligible voters, turnout, YES/NO/ABSTAIN counts and any conflicts. Keep personal vote choices private.
5. Confirm the quorum and formally vote on the rules **before** any constitutional vote. If the founders amend the rules, re-notice where necessary.
6. Debate the founding declaration and proposed constitution; if the text changes materially, defer the vote and recirculate the fixed version.
7. Record the ballot result accurately. A passed motion may be recorded as adopted only when the approved procedure was actually followed and the result verified.
