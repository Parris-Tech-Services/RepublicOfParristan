# Fred — first steps as Treasurer (about 20 minutes)

## 1. Open the ledger
Download [PARRISTAN_LEDGER_2026-27.ods](../../../templates/treasury/PARRISTAN_LEDGER_2026-27.ods) (opens in LibreOffice Calc, Excel or Google Sheets). Save a copy **privately** — your working copy may get notes you don't want public.

It has three sheets:
- **Ledger** — the 19 November payments are already entered as donation + expense pairs.
- **Budget 2026-27** — draft lines; "spent to date" fills in automatically from the ledger.
- **Monthly checks** — one row per month for you and a second officer to sign off.

## 2. Fill in the blanks on the Ledger sheet
Ask whoever paid (Josh paid the domain; ask who paid Purelymail):
- their name → "Paid by / from" column (replace `[name]`)
- the **Australian-dollar** amount from their card statement → put it in **Money in** on the donation row and **Money out** on the matching expense row.

The balance stays at $0.00: the costs were paid directly by members as donations. Keep receipts privately; never write card or bank numbers in the ledger.

## 3. Fill in the budget
Decide amounts for Printing, Symbols and Contingency (suggested: $20, $40, $20), and the voluntary contribution per member (suggested: $0 for now — the Assembly can decide later). Totals calculate themselves.

## 4. Second-officer check
Ask Jane to look over the November row on **Monthly checks** and put her name and the date in.

## 5. Bring to the first meeting
Read out [TREASURERS_REPORT_DEC_2026.md](TREASURERS_REPORT_DEC_2026.md) (fill in the AUD figures) and move that the Assembly adopts the budget.
