# Matthew Walker — Returning Officer pack

**Target:** send the notice of election on **Friday 20 November 2026**, before nominations open. Full kit with ballot papers and result form: [../drafts/ELECTION_KIT.md](../drafts/ELECTION_KIT.md).

## Step 1 — Decide three things (5 minutes)
1. **Opening and closing times.** Suggested: nominations open **20 Nov 12:00 pm**, close **27 Nov 5:00 pm**; voting opens **28 Nov 9:00 am**, closes **1 Dec 5:00 pm** (all AEDT).
2. **Ballot method.** With five voters, pick one:
   - **Confidential email ballot (simplest):** each voter emails their vote to `returningofficer@parristan.org`. You see the votes but promise never to reveal anyone's choice and publish totals only. *Not fully secret from you* — all members must agree to this in the notice period.
   - **Paper ballot in person:** only if everyone can meet between 28 Nov and 1 Dec. Fully secret.
3. **Self-nomination allowed?** Suggested: yes, with one other member seconding.

## Step 2 — Send this notice
Send from `returningofficer@parristan.org` to `assembly@parristan.org`, **and** post it in the Matrix room.

> **Subject: Notice of the First Presidential Election**
>
> I, Matthew Walker, Returning Officer (Gazette 2026-007), give notice of the election of the first President of the Republic of Parristan.
>
> **Office:** President, term of two years from the declaration of the result.
> **Voters:** the five founding members — Joshua Parris, Glenn Peterson, Jane Carbunkle, Fred Carbunkle and Matthew Walker. One vote each.
> **Candidates:** any founding member except the Returning Officer.
>
> **Nominations:** open Friday 20 November 2026, 12:00 pm; close Friday 27 November 2026, 5:00 pm AEDT.
> **How to nominate:** email returningofficer@parristan.org with your name, the name of a member who seconds you, and a candidate statement of up to 300 words. The seconder must confirm by email.
> **Candidate statements** will be sent to all members together on 27 November after nominations close.
>
> **Voting:** opens Saturday 28 November 2026, 9:00 am; closes Tuesday 1 December 2026, 5:00 pm AEDT.
> **How to vote:** email your vote to returningofficer@parristan.org. I will keep every individual vote confidential and publish only the totals. If any member objects to this method by 24 November, I will arrange a paper ballot instead.
> **If only one candidate:** a YES/NO confirmation vote. The candidate is confirmed only if YES is more than half of the votes cast.
> **Count and result:** within 24 hours of the close of voting, by email and in the Matrix room. Objections may be lodged with me within 48 hours of the result.
>
> Matthew Walker
> Returning Officer, Republic of Parristan
