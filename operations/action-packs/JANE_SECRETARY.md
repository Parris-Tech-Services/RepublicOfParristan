# Jane Carbunkle — Secretary pack (first monthly meeting)

Standing procedures (Gazette 2026-009) say meetings are monthly, arranged by the Secretary.

## Step 1 — Pick a date
Avoid the election (28 Nov – 1 Dec) so the new President can attend. Suggested: **a weeknight in the week of 7 December 2026**, 7:30 pm AEDT, by video call or in person. Post a quick poll in Matrix with two or three options.

## Step 2 — Send the notice (at least 24 hours before; aim for a week)
From `secretary@parristan.org` to `assembly@parristan.org`, and post in Matrix:

> **Subject: Notice — First Monthly Assembly Meeting, [day] [date] December 2026**
>
> The first monthly meeting of the Assembly will be held on **[day, date] at [time] AEDT** at **[place / video link]**.
>
> **Agenda**
> 1. Welcome and apologies
> 2. Confirm minutes of 19 November 2026
> 3. Result of the presidential election (Returning Officer)
> 4. President's draft mission and first-year goals (due within 14 days of election — Gazette 2026-008)
> 5. Treasurer's report: ledger to date and draft budget
> 6. Registrar's report: membership register and policy
> 7. CDO's report: email in service; website status
> 8. National symbols: decide whether to hold the symbols vote
> 9. Other business
> 10. Date of next meeting
>
> Please send items for "other business" to secretary@parristan.org by [date].
>
> Jane Carbunkle, Secretary

## Step 3 — At the meeting
Use the minutes template: [../../templates/ASSEMBLY_MINUTES.md](../../templates/ASSEMBLY_MINUTES.md). Record: who attended, each motion, the vote counts, and actions with owners and dates. No phone numbers or addresses in minutes.
