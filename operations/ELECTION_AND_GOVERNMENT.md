# First Government and Election Operations

**Status:** Voting procedures drafted; no President, Assembly leadership or cabinet is recorded as elected. The project organiser is not made President automatically.

## Institutional structure under the adopted constitution (with proposed election procedures)

- **Assembly:** all eligible adult citizen-members, one person one vote; sets policy and approves budgets.
- **President:** elected for a proposed two-year term, implements lawful internal decisions and is accountable to the Assembly.
- **Registrar/Secretary:** nominated or appointed under adopted procedures; maintains records and minutes.
- **Treasurer:** keeps a ledger and reports, with dual approvals for material spending where possible.
- **Electoral teller:** records turnout, checks eligibility, protects secret ballots and documents the count.
- **Appeals panel:** formed when sufficient unrelated members are available; must disclose conflicts.

With only five founders, people may need to combine roles. Publish conflicts, avoid a candidate independently certifying their own election, and obtain at least two people to check a tally.

## Vacancies and accountability

An officer may only act within the powers established by adopted documents. Refer removals, disputes and interim vacancies to the constitution and published standing orders; no person may acquire permanent office by unilateral GitHub edits.
