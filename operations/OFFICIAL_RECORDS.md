# Official Records and Evidence Controls

**Prepared:** 19 November 2026 · **Current state:** operational templates exist, but no ratified constitution, elected offices, recorded budget or official recognition is claimed.

## Records required for a functioning association

| Register | Who maintains it | What becomes public |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Constitution and amendments | Secretary | Complete adopted text, vote result, effective date, supersession history |
| Founding assembly | Secretary and chair | Approved minutes and aggregate votes without personal ballots |
| Elections | Independent teller | Eligible voter count, turnout, candidate totals, winners and terms |
| Membership | Registrar | Aggregate membership only; private identifiable records stay protected |
| Public decisions | Secretary | Motion number, authorised decision-maker, quorum, counts, date and evidence |
| Financial accounts | Treasurer | Approved budget and appropriate aggregate financial report |
| National symbols | Secretary | Adopted asset versions, design rights and adoption motions |
| Territory/tenure | Legal/administrative officer | Accurate lawful status and permission; redact private landholder details |
| External relations | Authorised liaison | Real agreements and appropriately releasable correspondence |
| Corrections | Gazette editor | Dated errata and links to original notices |

## Status rules

- **PROPOSED:** document is an invitation or draft.
- **APPROVED PROCEDURE:** the actual competent people adopted the process, but not necessarily the underlying proposal.
- **ADOPTED:** authentic decision and evidence exists, with effective date.
- **IN FORCE:** adopted act has commenced under its actual terms.
- **SUPERSEDED:** later lawful instrument replaces it; retain history.
- **NOT VERIFIED:** a reported event has not yet produced sufficient reliable record.

GitHub merges are document edits; they cannot substitute for a real meeting, election, land transaction or recognition.
