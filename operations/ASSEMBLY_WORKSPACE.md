# Assembly workspace

Open the [private Assembly workspace](https://intranet.parristan.org/assembly) with your Parristan office email.

- **Meeting and minutes:** create a meeting draft. Record the Sydney date, notice actually issued, agenda, papers, attendance, quorum and minutes. Submit the draft to the Secretary. The Secretary records the Assembly approval reference or returns the draft for changes. Meetings are visible to all signed-in offices.
- **Restricted document:** select one collaborating office. Both offices can save draft text revisions and read revision history. The creator submits a version for review and the collaborator approves it or returns it with a reason.
- **Approval request:** select a reviewing office. Describe the spending, access or publication request in the draft. The selected office can approve or return the submitted record. Record the actual delegated authority separately where required.

Use the official notice channels and monthly or extra-meeting rules in [Gazette 2026-009](../gazette/2026/2026-009-standing-procedures.md) and [Gazette 2026-011](../gazette/2026/2026-011-official-email-live.md). The workspace records actions and approvals. Issue formal notices and publish authorised minutes through the existing channels.

The restricted document workflow supports text and saved revisions. The existing work-register export does not include restricted Assembly records. Their recovery still needs a private backup procedure and a verified restore.

## Voting and additional services

The first presidential outcome remains [Gazette 2026-013](../gazette/2026/2026-013-first-president-confirmed.md). The [private voting application](../apps/private-voting/README.md) has separate hosting and database requirements. Future official ballots need a Returning Officer-approved roll and method, verified founder access, and a reviewed secrecy design. Office email access is independent of electoral eligibility.

Payroll, payments and public-service workflows remain tracked in the [infrastructure checklist](../DIGITAL_INFRASTRUCTURE_TODO.md) pending the respective approved service and operator.
