# Legal form comparison (DRAFT — not legal advice)

**For:** the founders. **Status:** background to support a decision. Check current rules with NSW Fair Trading or a lawyer before acting. See also the existing [NSW association pathway](../NSW_ASSOCIATION_PATHWAY.md).

None of these options creates sovereignty. They are ways for a group to hold money, sign agreements and limit personal liability inside Australian law.

| Option | Cost (approx.) | Members needed | Liability protection | Admin burden | Good for |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Stay unincorporated** | $0 | Any | None — members personally liable | Very low | Now, while there is no money or property |
| **NSW incorporated association** | Modest application fee; check current fee | At least 5 (you have 5) | Yes | Annual summary, public officer, committee, financial records | Holding money, a bank account, insurance, a venue agreement |
| **Company limited by guarantee (ACNC/ASIC)** | Higher, plus ongoing fees | 1+ | Yes | High | Larger, national or charity operations |
| **Co-operative** | Moderate | 5+ | Yes | Medium–high | Shared trading/business activity |

## Suggested approach
1. **Now:** stay unincorporated; no money or property held.
2. **Trigger to revisit:** when you want a bank account, hold more than about $[500], rent a venue, or need insurance.
3. **Then:** NSW incorporated association is the closest fit. Steps: adopt association rules (model rules or your own adapted from the Constitution), choose a public officer, apply online with NSW Fair Trading.

## Questions for a lawyer or NSW Fair Trading
- Can the association's name include "Republic"? (Some words are restricted.)
- Can the Constitution be used as the association's rules, or must they be separate?
- What happens to assets if the association winds up?
