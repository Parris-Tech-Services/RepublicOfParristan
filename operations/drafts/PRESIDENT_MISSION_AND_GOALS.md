**For:** President Joshua Parris to edit and bring to the Assembly. **Due:** by 3 December 2026 (Gazette 2026-008: within 14 days of the election). **Status:** draft — not adopted. Built from the Constitution (Article I), the draft Founding Declaration and the open items in `NATIONAL_OPERATIONS_TODO.md`.

## Mission (one paragraph)

> The Republic of Parristan is a small, peaceful, voluntary micronation that practises real democracy on a human scale. We govern ourselves by a written constitution, keep honest public records, run our affairs with open-source tools, and use what we learn to serve our members, our families and our local community. We are truthful about what we are: a civic association whose members remain subject to Australian law, not a recognised state.

## Values
Honesty in every public claim · Fair votes and open records · Privacy for members · Open-source, open-format technology · Service and learning, especially for junior members.
