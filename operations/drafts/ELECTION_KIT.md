## 3. Secret ballot — pick one method and announce it with the notice

With five voters, anonymity is hard. Choose the method members trust most.

| Method | How | Secrecy | Effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Paper ballot at a meeting (recommended if you can meet)** | Identical slips, folded, into a box; RO and one scrutineer count openly | Strong | Low |
| **B. Sealed envelopes** | Ballot slip in a blank inner envelope inside a named outer envelope; RO ticks the roll, then mixes inner envelopes before opening | Strong | Medium |
| **C. Anonymous online form with one-time codes** | RO gives each voter a random code privately; form asks only code + choice; RO checks codes are valid and used once | Good if the RO does not keep the code list linked to names after handing out codes | Medium |
| D. Private message to RO | Each voter messages the RO | **Not secret from the RO.** Use only if all members agree in writing | Low |

## 4. Ballot paper

**Contested election (two or more candidates):**
> First Presidential Election, Republic of Parristan. Vote for ONE candidate by marking one box.
> ☐ [Candidate A] ☐ [Candidate B]

The candidate must receive **more than half of valid votes cast** under the proposed rules. Otherwise conduct a runoff between the two leading candidates, with dates and tie-handling announced by the Returning Officer under an approved procedure.

**Single valid candidate — confirmation ballot:**
> Should [Candidate name] be confirmed as the first President of the Republic of Parristan?
> ☐ YES ☐ NO

Confirmed only if YES is more than half of valid votes cast. If not confirmed: [nominations reopen for 7 days].

Abstentions and blank or unclear ballots are recorded as informal, not counted as YES or NO.
