# External correspondence protocol (DRAFT)

**For:** the Assembly to adopt; the President (once elected) to operate. **Status:** draft. See also [diplomacy](../../docs/DIPLOMACY.md).

## 1. Rules
1. Only the President, or an officer the Assembly names, sends letters on behalf of Parristan.
2. Every outgoing letter is approved by [the President / two officers] before sending.
3. Never claim recognition, treaties or status that do not exist. Describe Parristan as "a micronation founded in 2026 by five members in New South Wales".
4. No personal addresses in letters. Use the official channel or a role email address once one exists.
5. Every letter sent or received is logged (section 3). Copies kept privately; a summary may be published.
6. A friendly reply is not recognition. Only record "mutual recognition" if both sides have written it down and the Assembly approved it.

## 2. First-contact letter
> Dear [name / title],
>
> I write on behalf of the Republic of Parristan, a micronation founded on 19 November 2026 by five members in New South Wales, Australia. We adopted a constitution at our founding assembly and are holding our first presidential election this month.
>
> We admire [specific thing about their project] and would welcome a friendly exchange of ideas about [governance / open-source government / community projects].
>
> We do not claim recognition by any government and do not ask for any formal agreement at this stage. Our public Gazette is at [website address].
>
> Yours sincerely,
> [Name], [Title], on behalf of the Republic of Parristan
> [Date]
