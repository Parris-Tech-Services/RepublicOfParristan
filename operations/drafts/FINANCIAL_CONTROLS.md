# Financial controls (DRAFT)

**For:** Fred Carbunkle, Treasurer (Gazette 2026-008), then an Assembly vote. **Status:** draft. Currently no money is held.

## 1. Principles
- Money belongs to the association, never mixed with personal money.
- Every payment has a reason, an approval and a receipt.
- Two people see every transaction.

## 2. Spending limits
| Amount (AUD) | Who approves |
|---|---|
| Up to $[50] | Treasurer, within the adopted budget |
| $[50]–$[250] | Treasurer + one other officer |
| Over $[250] or outside budget | Assembly vote |

## 4. Ledger (spreadsheet columns, `.ods` file)
Date · Description · Category · In · Out · Running balance · Approved by · Receipt reference

## 5. Monthly check
- Treasurer updates the ledger.
- A second officer (not the Treasurer) checks it against receipts/bank and initials it.
- Balance reported at the next Assembly meeting.

## 6. Banking
Until the association is incorporated, do not open an account in the association's name. If money must be held, one officer holds it in a separate, clearly labelled account and the ledger is the record. Revisit after the legal form decision.

## 7. Conflict-of-interest policy
1. Any officer who could gain personally from a decision (money, a contract, a family member's benefit) must say so before discussion.
2. That officer does not vote on it.
3. The declaration is recorded in the minutes.
4. A register of declared interests is kept by the Secretary.
