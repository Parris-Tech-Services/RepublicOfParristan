# National symbols — ballot, specifications and register (DRAFT)

**For:** the Assembly. **Status:** draft. Proposals are in [symbols adoption pack](../SYMBOLS_ADOPTION.md). Nothing is adopted until a recorded vote.

## 1. Adoption ballot
> **Republic of Parristan — National symbols ballot.** Mark one box per question.
> 1. Adopt the *Founding Star* flag as the national flag? ☐ YES ☐ NO ☐ ABSTAIN
> 2. Adopt the civic arms proposal as the coat of arms? ☐ YES ☐ NO ☐ ABSTAIN
> 3. Adopt "Liberty · Service · Truth" as the national motto? ☐ YES ☐ NO ☐ ABSTAIN
> 4. Adopt the anthem lyrics in the anthem proposal (melody to be decided later)? ☐ YES ☐ NO ☐ ABSTAIN

Each question passes with more than half of valid votes cast.

## 3. Anthem melody — options
- A member composes one; or
- A melody in the public domain is chosen; or
- A musician is commissioned with a written licence giving the association rights to use it.
Record the composer and licence in the register.

## 4. Use policy
Members may use adopted symbols for non-commercial, truthful purposes. No use that suggests Australian government endorsement or sovereign recognition.
