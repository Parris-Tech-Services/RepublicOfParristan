> **Motion 7 (en bloc) — The President's first programme**
>
> That the Assembly adopts the following:
>
> 1. **Mission and goals.** The mission statement and five first-year goals in operations/drafts/PRESIDENT_MISSION_AND_GOALS.md. The President reports progress at each monthly meeting.
> 2. **Chair and minutes.** The President chairs Assembly meetings and the Secretary takes minutes. If the President is absent, members present choose a chair.
> 3. **Channels.** Votes and debate take place in the Matrix room. Notices and external correspondence go by official email (assembly@parristan.org and office addresses).
> 4. **First monthly meeting.** The Secretary sets a date in the week of 7 December 2026 by poll.
> 5. **Founding costs.** The US$18.50 paid on 19 November 2026 for the parristan.org domain and official email is recorded by the Treasurer as a donation from the members who paid, not to be reimbursed.
> 6. **Financial controls.** The Financial Controls in operations/drafts/FINANCIAL_CONTROLS.md are adopted: up to $50 by the Treasurer within budget; $50–$250 by the Treasurer plus one other officer; above $250 or outside budget by Assembly vote. No bank account is opened until the Assembly decides one is needed.
> 7. **Membership policy.** The Membership Policy, Privacy Notice and appeals process in operations/drafts/MEMBERSHIP_POLICY.md are adopted. Members under 18 may join as junior members with a parent or guardian's consent, without voting rights.
> 8. **Website address.** The official website is moved to parristan.org.
> 9. **Symbols vote.** The Assembly votes on the flag, coat of arms and motto, as separate questions, in January 2027. Anthem melody ideas are collected meanwhile.
> 10. **Growth.** Membership applications open to invited people from about March 2027, once the register is working.
>
> Vote YES / NO / ABSTAIN. Closes after 48 hours or when all five have voted.
