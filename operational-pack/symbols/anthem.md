# National Anthem: "Red Dirt, Gold Wheat"

**Status:** Adopted pending First Assembly vote
**Tune:** Sung to any tune the assembled citizens can agree on. Default: clapped rhythm, slow-slow-quick-quick-slow.

---

**Verse 1**
From the red dirt to the gold wheat,
Where the long road meets the sky,
We are Parristan together,
And we wave at utes going by.

**Verse 2**
When the gauge says not a millimetre,
And the paddocks crack and burn,
We will share what's in the cupboard,
And we'll wait for rain's return.

**Chorus**
Fix it, mend it, lend a hand,
Brave and kind in Parristan!
Spanner, crook and sausage high,
Under one wide western sky.

**Verse 3 (sung only on National Day)**
Bedtime may be late tonight,
By decree the stars may stay,
Lift your cordial, lift your voices,
Happy Founding, Parristan Day!

---

*Etiquette:* Citizens stand for the chorus. Anyone holding a sausage may remain seated.
