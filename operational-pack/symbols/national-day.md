# National Day: Founding Day

**Date:** 19 November, every year
**First observed:** 19 November 2026

## Order of the Day

1. **Morning:** Official rain gauge reading by the Minister for Agriculture & Weather Announcements. All citizens stand in silence.
2. **Midday:** National Sausage Sizzle. Payment accepted in Parri.
3. **Afternoon:** Founding Games (see `culture/traditions.md`).
4. **Evening:** Anthem, presidential address (maximum 3 minutes), awarding of the Order of the Spanner.
5. **Bedtime:** Extended by presidential decree.

## Honours

- **Order of the Spanner:** for fixing something nobody asked you to fix.
- **Golden Gauge:** for the most accurate rain prediction of the year.
- **Red Pen Medal:** awarded by the Treasurer for the year's finest correct use of grammar.
