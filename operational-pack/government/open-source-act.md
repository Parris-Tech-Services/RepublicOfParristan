# Open Source Act 2026

*An Act to secure the digital sovereignty of the Republic of Parristan.*
**Status:** Adopted 19 November 2026 (organiser-reported; see [correction to the minutes](../../docs/FOUNDING_ASSEMBLY_2026-11-19.md)). A [consolidated proposal](../../docs/OPEN_SOURCE_ACT_CONSOLIDATED_PROPOSAL.md) would replace this text and the governance version once voted on.

## 1. Short title
This Act may be cited as the *Open Source Act 2026*.

## 2. Inspiration
This Act follows the example of the French Gendarmerie nationale (GendBuntu) and the German state of Schleswig-Holstein (Open Innovation and Open Source Strategy).

## 3. National systems
1. All national systems of the Republic shall run on free and open-source software wherever a workable option exists.
2. Proprietary software may be used only where no open alternative exists, and only with written approval of the Office of Sovereign Systems.

## 4. Open formats
1. National documents shall be stored in OpenDocument formats (`.odt`, `.ods`, `.odp`) or plain text / Markdown.
2. Images in PNG or SVG. Data in CSV or JSON.

## 5. Office of Sovereign Systems (OSPO)
1. The Office of Sovereign Systems is established within the Ministry for Infrastructure, Utes & Things That Rattle.
2. The President appoints a Chief Digital Officer to run it.
3. The Office maintains the *Sovereign IT Checklist* (`digital/sovereign-it-checklist.md`) and reports progress at each Assembly.

## 6. Data stays home
National data shall be hosted on equipment owned by the Republic or its citizens (The Shed), with backups kept on Republic-owned storage.

## 7. Privacy
No national system shall publish a citizen's home address, phone number or personal email without that citizen's written consent.

## 8. Commencement
This Act commences on passage by the Assembly.
