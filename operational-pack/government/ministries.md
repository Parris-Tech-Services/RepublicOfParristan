## Reserved Junior Ministries

These offices are reserved for junior citizens and are appointed by the President:

- **Minister for Snacks:** oversees the national pantry and sausage sizzle supply.
- **Minister for Bedtime Appeals:** may lodge one appeal per night for extended bedtime. The President decides.
- **Minister for Games & Fun:** runs the Founding Games and chooses the casting-vote game in disputes.
- **Minister for Animals:** responsible for the wellbeing of all pets, chooks and visiting galahs.

## Appointing a New Minister

1. Any citizen may propose a new ministry at a National Assembly.
2. The proposal needs a seconder.
3. A simple majority of founders present approves it.
4. The President appoints the holder. The Treasurer records it in the register.
5. The new Minister takes the oath:

> I will serve the Republic with a cheerful heart, a helpful hand, and a clean workbench.

## Removal from Office

A Minister may be removed by a two-thirds vote of the Assembly, or immediately by the Treasurer for repeated crimes against grammar.
