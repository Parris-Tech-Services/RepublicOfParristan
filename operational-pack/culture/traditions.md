# National Traditions of Parristan

## Daily
- **The Reading.** Each morning the rain gauge is read aloud. Anyone present responds: *"May it fill."*
- **The Wave.** Citizens wave at every passing ute. Two-finger steering-wheel waves count.

## Weekly
- **Shed Saturday.** One hour in The Shed fixing, building or tinkering. Every citizen is welcome, no skill needed.
- **Game Night.** The Minister for Games & Fun picks the game. Losers do the dishes.

## Annual Calendar

| Date | Occasion |
|---|---|
| 19 November | **Founding Day** (see `symbols/national-day.md`) |
| First Saturday in March | **Harvest Fair**: best vegetable, best scone, best rain forecast |
| June long weekend | **The Great LAN**: national gaming tournament |
| First rain after a dry spell | **Rain Day**: declared by the Minister for Agriculture; everyone goes outside |
| Date set by the Treasurer | **Audit Day**: Treasury counts the Parri, followed by cake |

## The Founding Games
Held each Founding Day:
1. **Tyre Roll**: roll an old tyre the furthest in a straight line.
2. **Gauge Guess**: guess the year's total rainfall; closest wins the Golden Gauge.
3. **Spanner Sprint**: relay race carrying a (safe, light) spanner.
4. **Grammar Bee**: the Treasurer presides. No mercy.
5. **Sausage Toss**: underarm, into a bucket. The sausages are eaten afterwards.
