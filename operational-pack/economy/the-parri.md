# The Parri (₱R)

**Official currency of the Republic of Parristan**
**Issuer:** The Treasury (Treasurer: Jane Carbunkle)
**Peg:** 1 Parri = 1 sausage sizzle

## Denominations

| Note | Colour | Features |
|---|---|---|
| ₱R 1 | Wheat gold | The rain gauge |
| ₱R 5 | Red dirt | The Indicator |
| ₱R 10 | Galah pink | Two galahs |
| ₱R 20 | Sky blue | The Shed |

## Treasury Rules

1. Only the Treasury may print Parri.
2. Notes must be stamped or signed by the Treasurer to be legal tender.
3. Forged Parri are confiscated and the forger owes the Republic one sincere apology.
4. The President may declare a Parri bonus on National Day.
5. In-game, the Parri also functions as the game currency (see `game/lore.json`).
