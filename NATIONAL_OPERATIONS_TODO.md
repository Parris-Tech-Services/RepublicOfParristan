## Status meanings

- **Done:** the named concrete deliverable exists and has been verified; not necessarily adopted as policy.
- **Requires founders:** a meeting, appointment, vote, signed agreement or other human-authorised act is required.
- **Requires external party:** a public authority, landowner, third-party institution or foreign state must act.
- **Not applicable yet:** a prerequisite has not been satisfied.

## Completion gates

- **Operational micronation:** adopted constitution, elected/appointed offices under valid rules, functioning citizenship administration, ongoing decisions/records, actual lawful operations and accountable finances.
- **Sovereign state:** a separate and far more demanding matter involving effective population, territory, government, external relations and relevant international law and recognition. No assurance of statehood is made.

Never copy personal addresses, phone numbers, identity documents, member ballots or private databases into either GitHub repository.
