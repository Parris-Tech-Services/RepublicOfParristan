# Governance and Official Records

**Status (19 November 2026):** The founding assembly unanimously ratified the internal constitution and appointed Glenn Peterson as Chief Digital Officer. Subsequent written votes appointed a Returning Officer and interim Secretary, Treasurer and Registrar. **No President has been elected.**

## Founding phase

An organising convenor may coordinate drafts and meetings, but may not present themself as a duly elected President unless an election has occurred under ratified rules.

## Proposed democratic workflow

1. Submit a written proposal with a public summary and an internal tracking reference.
2. Identify impacts on rights, finances, privacy, existing law and other commitments.
3. Allow a consultation period before voting.
4. Publish eligibility and quorum rules in advance.
5. Conduct a verifiable, fair vote.
6. Record the result and, only after valid adoption, place the adopted instrument in an official directory.
7. Review procedures annually.

GitHub pull requests may document proposals but **merging code is not itself a valid constitutional vote** unless explicitly authorised by duly adopted procedures.

## Records to establish

- Constituent assembly minutes
- Register of adopted laws and version history
- Election results and offices
- Financial ledger and approved budgets
- Conflicts of interest disclosures
- External correspondence log
- Membership totals (aggregate only)

## Separation of powers

The adopted constitution (v0.1 subsequently confirmed by the five founders in Motion 4, with original notice time still unrecorded) envisions an Assembly, an elected President and an independent internal appeals panel. The panel's power would extend only to lawful association matters.
