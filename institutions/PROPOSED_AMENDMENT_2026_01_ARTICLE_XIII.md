# Proposed Constitutional Amendment 2026-01 — Article XIII: Civic and Simulated Services

**Status:** FULL-TEXT PUBLICATION AND CONSULTATION ONLY — NOT ADOPTED
**Proposed:** 23 November 2026 under ordinary resolution PA-2026-001, clause 9.
**Text version:** 2026-01, first published copy; subsequent substantive revisions require a fresh fixed full-text notice.
**Notice record:** [Gazette 2026-017](../gazette/2026/2026-017-article-xiii-amendment-notice.md).
**Constitution:** [v0.1 (unchanged)](CONSTITUTION.md), Articles I–XII.
**Authority to submit amendment:** Constitution Article XI.1; proposer President Joshua Parris. **No amendment ballot has occurred.**

## Complete text proposed to be added

The Constitution is proposed to be amended by inserting the following Article XIII immediately after Article XII, without deleting or replacing the text of Articles I–XII:

> ## Article XIII — Civic and Simulated Services
>
> **XIII.1 — Direction of adaptation.** Parristan's internal rules shall adapt to applicable Australian law and the lawful institutions of the places in which its members reside.
>
> **XIII.2 — Separation of worlds.** Fictional territories, simulated citizens, governments, courts, enforcement services and finances shall have no real-world public-law authority or legal effect.
>
> **XIII.3 — Voluntary civic services.** Parristan may organise lawful voluntary member associations, chapters and services, subject to consent, privacy protections, approved financial controls and applicable law.
>
> **XIII.4 — Preservation of rights.** Nothing in this Article overrides existing constitutional rights, confers sovereign or police powers, authorises compulsory land charges or reduces the protections governing constitutional amendments.

No other constitutional wording is proposed to change.

## Explanatory memorandum

This proposal expressly distinguishes Parristan's real human-member association from simulated settlements and residents integrated with Repository Town. It preserves the precedence of Australian and NSW law, protects real property and non-members, and permits lawful voluntary member services without creating a court, council, state, police force or real tax.

**Effect before ratification:** none as constitutional text. Similar principles already apply as ordinary policy under PA-2026-001 and existing Articles I, III, VII, IX and X, to the extent those Articles permit.

## Article XI requirements — independently verifiable

1. Make the complete fixed text accessible to eligible members and the public, recording when the approved official **notice is delivered**. A repository publication alone must not be mistaken for proof members actually received the notice.
2. Allow at least **14 full days of notice** between valid publication/delivery and the amendment vote. A deliberately conservative target is **8 December 2026 or later** if delivery is established on 23 November; if delivery occurs later, move the ballot accordingly.
3. Verify eligibility, defined quorum, and an affirmative vote of **at least two-thirds of valid votes cast**. Existing ordinary-motion early-closure rules do **not** waive the separate amendment notice.
4. Record the actual result, turnout, abstentions, official notice time, adoption/effective date and precise text version before updating the Constitution. If unsuccessful, retain this document as a rejected or withdrawn proposal rather than silently altering the Constitution.
