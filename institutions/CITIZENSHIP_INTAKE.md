# Private Citizenship / Membership Intake — Setup Specification

**NOT OPEN. No application data is being accepted in this public repository.**

Citizenship of Parristan, if adopted, means **voluntary membership** in the micronation. It is not nationality recognised by the Australian government or a right to passports, immigration status or exemptions.

Before accepting applicants, establish:
- An adopted eligibility and membership policy, including appeals and withdrawal.
- A secure private intake and access-controlled register.
- A clear privacy notice and data retention/deletion arrangements.
- Minimum necessary information: chosen public name, private contact method, consent, application date and status.
- A deduplicated voting mechanism that respects privacy.
- Approved communication arrangements and an ability to withdraw.

Do **not** publish names, home addresses, identity documents, phone numbers, dates of birth or other sensitive data on GitHub or the website.

Public GitHub issues may be used **only for general policy feedback**, not for membership applications. Until the system is established, inquiries about citizenship are expressions of interest only and confer no rights or status.
