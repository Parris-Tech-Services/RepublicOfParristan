# First Constituent Assembly — convening proposal

**Historical convening proposal, prepared 19 November 2026.** The organiser subsequently reported the meeting **was held at 3:30 pm AEDT on 19 November 2026 with all five founders present**. See [assembly report](../docs/FOUNDING_ASSEMBLY_2026-11-19.md). A formal ratification tally is still pending.

The purpose of a first constituent assembly is to bring real, consenting participants together to consider the founding declaration, deliberate on the draft constitution and, if they choose, vote on formally adopting a constitution for Parristan's voluntary micronational community.

## Proposed arrangements

| Question | Proposal |
| --- | --- |
| Time and place | To be agreed by actual participants; no date announced |
| Format | Accessible in-person or online meeting, with lawful venue access |
| Convenor | Founding project organiser, solely to arrange the meeting; not the President |
| Eligible voters | Distinct adult people who consent to participate in the founding process |
| Quorum | At least three eligible adult participants, including at least two other than the convenor |
| Notice | At least fourteen full days after the final constitution text and rules are circulated |
| Decision | Proposed two-thirds majority of YES over YES+NO, provided quorum is met |

These rules are **proposals**, not automatically binding. Prospective founders should agree on the rules *before* the final notice and ballot.

## Agenda

1. Establish quorum and approve minutes and chair.
2. Disclose conflicts of interest and verify that participation is voluntary.
3. Review the founding declaration and purposes.
4. Consider and amend the circulated constitution.
5. If the full text has been circulated with adequate notice, hold the ratification vote.
6. If ratified, agree on a first election timetable and lawful administrative arrangements.
7. Discuss membership intake, symbols, website, physical meetings and next steps.
8. Publish signed-off minutes without private attendee details.

## Public consultation

[Discussion and suggestions for the founding assembly](https://github.com/Parris-Tech-Services/RepublicOfParristan/issues/1).

A GitHub comment is **not** a ballot or an application for citizenship. Any actual participants and voters must be recruited, invited, consented and verified through a **private** process. The public site and GitHub issue are not places for personal details.
