# Proposed Constitutional Ratification Procedure

**Historical proposal, superseded by founder-reported Assembly decisions on 19 November 2026.** The 5/5 founders unanimously amended the **founding-assembly** notice proposal from 14 days to 1 hour (5–0) *before* unanimously ratifying the Constitution (5–0), effective 19 November 2026. See [minutes](../docs/FOUNDING_ASSEMBLY_2026-11-19.md) and [return](../docs/ASSEMBLY_RATIFICATION_RETURN_2026-11-19.md). The wording below is preserved as the **original proposal**, not an assertion that the old 14-day draft rule was used. Exact notice delivery time is not recorded. The constitutional **Article XI** remains a separate 14-day requirement for future amendments.

## Notice and eligibility

- Announce the meeting date, time zone, access method, voting rules, quorum and electorate definition in advance.
- Circulate a complete **fixed version** of the constitution, identified by Git commit SHA.
- Allow a proposed minimum of **14 full days** after circulating that fixed version before the vote. Material changes require updated notice.
- Eligible voters are distinct, consenting adult people admitted to the founding-process electorate; GitHub accounts or bots cannot create extra votes.
- Keep the verified voting list private. Publicly record **aggregate counts**, never identifying voting records.

## Proposed quorum and vote threshold

- Proposed quorum: **three** verified eligible adults, at least two other than the convenor.
- Question: “Shall the proposed Constitution of the Republic of Parristan, version [SHA], be adopted as the voluntary internal constitution of this developing micronational community?”
- Ballot: **YES / NO / ABSTAIN**.
- Approval: at least **two-thirds of YES over YES+NO votes** where quorum is met.
- Abstentions may count toward attendance but not YES+NO votes.
- If no quorum or inadequate verification, **no vote is valid**; record the meeting as discussion only.

Founding participants must agree on and publish the operative rules *before* voting. The above thresholds are recommendations and must not be changed retrospectively.

## Integrity and publication

Use an impartial teller where possible; disclose unavoidable conflicts. Record the complete text, timestamp, number of eligible voters, turnout, abstentions, YES and NO counts, disputes and review signatures.

Only if ratification actually passes should the constitution be marked “ADOPTED” and its effective date registered. Never imply that merging a GitHub pull request equals constitutional adoption.
