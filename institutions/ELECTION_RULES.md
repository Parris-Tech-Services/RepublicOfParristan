# First Presidential Election — Proposed Rules

**Status: first-election rules still proposed; constitution ratified at the 19 November 2026 founding assembly (5–0, per minutes). The first President (Joshua Parris) was confirmed 5–0 on 19 November 2026 under the same-day procedure in Motion 6 ([Gazette 2026-012](../gazette/2026/2026-012-election-timetable-brought-forward.md)); these proposed rules were not used and remain proposals for future elections.**

The consolidated adopted constitution calls for a presidential election within **90 days of ratification**: by **17 February 2027** on the recorded 19 November 2026 effective date. An election date and final electoral regulations have not been announced.

1. Announce electorate eligibility and voting method before nominations open.
2. Allow all eligible adult citizen-members to nominate, subject to uniformly applied rules.
3. Open nominations for at least seven days; circulate candidate information and voting timetable to all eligible voters.
4. Conduct one-person-one-vote secret balloting where practicable. Multiple GitHub accounts do not mean multiple votes.
5. A candidate with more than half the valid votes wins. If none achieves this, conduct a runoff among the top two.
6. A sole candidate should face a YES/NO confirmation vote.
7. Use an independent teller where possible, disclose conflicts and allow a documented challenge period.
8. Publish verifiable **aggregate** results and term dates, without individual voting choices or unnecessary personal information.

A founder is **not automatically President**. A constitutionally authorised office is only filled after a real appointment or election has been verified.
