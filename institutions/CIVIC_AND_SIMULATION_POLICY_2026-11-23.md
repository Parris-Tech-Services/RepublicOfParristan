**Authority:** [Gazette 2026-016](../gazette/2026/2026-016-civic-governance-repository-town-integration.md), [Decision PA-2026-001](DECISION_REGISTER.md), Constitution v0.1 Articles I–XII. This is **not a constitutional amendment or Australian public law**.

## Binding classification rule

All Parristan activities and records must be accurately labelled as one of these distinct categories:

1. **Real association activity:** voluntary adult members, lawful member meetings, ordinary Assembly resolutions, approved finances, real contracts and real offices under Parristan's Constitution. Only consenting, verified humans admitted under policy may count as eligible members/voters. No AI resident has a vote.
2. **Fictional simulation:** AI citizens, settlement boundaries, virtual councils/local associations, police-shaped Civic Service characters, courts, businesses, local services, game fines/taxes/rates, and virtual currency. These create no real member, debt, property right, public power, nationality or obligation.
3. **Future proposal:** constitutional text, unapproved real expenditure, external agreements, territorial claims, incorporation and major real-world initiatives. These have no effect until separately authorised using the applicable process.

Public screens, dashboards, APIs, Gazette notices and source code must not conflate these categories.

## Immediate civic rules

**Rule C-01 — Submission to applicable law.** Australian Commonwealth, NSW and local law takes precedence. No Parristan rule overrides real property, planning, policing, taxation, emergency-service or land-access requirements.

**Rule C-02 — Local Civic Associations.** The fictional settlements may host volunteer civic associations for halls, community gardens, festivals, postal counters, libraries, local maintenance and community projects. The **Tealford Civic Association (Simulation)** is designated the *first chartered fictional association*, with zero real-world legal authority or budget. Additional fictional chapters may be created by simulation configuration; they do not become separate Australian bodies merely by appearing in-game.

**Rule C-03 — Local service accountability.** For a fictional association, record the settlement ID, simulated officer IDs (if actually populated), virtual service inventory, optional simulated budget, decisions, start/end dates and links to the associated game systems. An empty entry means *not yet implemented*, not staffed. Real human association chapters require separate member consent and an appropriate internal decision.

**Rule C-04 — Civic Service (Simulation).** NPCs may depict peaceful public safety, traffic assistance, lost property and mediation in-game. They are never presented as NSW Police, NSW Government staff or real officers; they cannot arrest, detain, search, confiscate, impose real fines or interfere with emergency services. Player actions in the simulation are fictional events only.

**Rule C-05 — Virtual economy.** "Parri" balances and any simulated wages, rates, rent, fines or levies have no convertibility or attachment to real property; no external payment requests may be generated by the simulation. No compulsory real charge is enacted by PA-2026-001. Real donations and service subscriptions require voluntary, informed consent, an approved real-world budget, and compliance with existing finance controls.

**Rule C-06 — Privacy and identity.** Never copy the private real membership register into the fictional world or create voting credentials from AI IDs. Preserve opt-in and minimal collection for real participants; synthetic agents must be distinguishable.

**Rule C-07 — Shared-world geography.** Repository Town's existing world, the representation of Narromine, and the fictional Parristan settlements (starting with Lantern Point south and Tealford nearby) use documented simulated coordinates and measured/compressed routes. These coordinates are game anchors, never assertions of ownership or surveyed boundaries. Nothing about distance from a real town proves the absence of real property, residents or environmental protections.

**Rule C-08 — Implementation standard.** Use a verified version-controlled design, believable NSW-inspired streets and traffic, shared Repository Town NPC renderer, persistent homes and jobs, safe pedestrian navigation, real test results, mobile performance checks, and actual browser-playtest verification before announcing a feature as working.

**Rule C-09 — No automatic government effect.** Cloud build, GitHub merge, AI agent output or simulation tick is not an Assembly vote, constitutional amendment, real appointment, public licence, legal order, or government grant.

**Rule C-10 — Notice and records.** The Secretary must authenticate the reported 23 November ordinary vote (eligibility, text, method, quorum, timestamp, full results and any meeting notice), then certify or correct Gazette No. 2026-016. The full proposed Article XIII is separately published for consultation and requires its **own** Article XI procedure.
