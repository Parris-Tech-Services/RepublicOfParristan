# Roadmap — Building the Republic of Parristan

This roadmap distinguishes **administrative progress**, **civic legitimacy** and **international statehood**. Completing internal milestones does not itself establish sovereign status.

- [x] Create an internal Enterprise organisation and a separate public Gazette
- [x] Publish an accurate public status statement, notices and draft founding documents
- [ ] Agree on a real-world mission, values and long-term objectives with prospective members

## Phase 3 — Civic and cultural identity

- [ ] Commission and adopt original flag, coat of arms and anthem
- [ ] Establish an official website with accurate and dated information
- [ ] Support voluntary public-service and educational projects
- [ ] Publish public reports on governance and participation

## Phase 4 — Physical presence and lawful territorial research

- [ ] Determine what physical headquarters or meeting space is useful
- [ ] Seek lawful owner permission and documented access
- [ ] Research land rights, tenure and legal feasibility
- [ ] Obtain qualified legal analysis before making any territorial claim

## Phase 5 — External relations and assessment

- [ ] Adopt a correspondence protocol
- [ ] Contact other micronations in good faith
- [ ] Maintain an evidence-backed external relations register
- [ ] Independently assess statehood aspirations and legal obstacles
- [ ] Update public claims only when objective evidence justifies them
