# Constitutional Amendment 2026-01 — Local Services and Simulation Authority

**Status:** TABLED by the President. 14-day notice running. Not adopted. Constitution version remains 0.1.
**Proposed by:** President Joshua Parris, as a citizen (Art XI.1: "Any citizen may propose an amendment with its full text publicly available before voting").
**Procedure:** Art XI.2 — at least **14 days' notice** and a **two-thirds majority of valid votes cast**, quorum per the Assembly.
**Versions:** v0.1 tabled 2026-11-23. Full text below is the text that must be voted on.

> This amendment changes Parristan's own internal rules only. It does not claim territory (Art IX.1), does not confer real legal power (Art X.1), and does not alter Australian law, NSW law or any real council. Its purpose is the opposite of overreach: Parristan's settlements need an internal civic form, because every institution they must live alongside — a real shire council, a real police station, the fictional Repository Town council — already has one.

## Problem statement

The Constitution gives Parristan a national layer (President, Assembly, Ethics and Appeals Panel) and no local layer at all. Article III.3 forbids compulsory charges without a constitutional authorisation that does not exist. Article VII.3 forbids any body from fining or imprisoning. Article IX.1 forbids territorial or jurisdictional claims. The result is that the twelve settlements are unwritten data: no officers, no budget, no services, no local records.

That is internally consistent but empirically unrealistic. A settlement of forty residents beside a shire council, a constabulary and a courthouse does not have zero institutions; it has small ones — a progress association, a hall committee, a volunteer brigade, a postal agency. This amendment authorises exactly that smallness, and bounds it.

## Amendment text

The Constitution is amended as follows.

### 1. Article III.3 is replaced

> **3.** Members may not be charged compulsory taxes or fees except under a decision that is (a) authorised by this constitution, (b) disclosed in full to the members affected, (c) consented to individually by each member charged, and (d) recorded in a published ledger. No charge may be imposed on a member who has not consented.

### 2. A new Article XIII is inserted after Article XII

> ## Article XIII — Local civic government, charges and simulation
>
> **1. Direction of adaptation.** Parristan's internal rules adapt to the law and institutions of the places where members live. No Parristan instrument may amend, override or purport to replace Australian law, NSW law, a real local government area or a real council.
>
> **2. Settlement Civic Associations.** Members associated with a settlement may constitute a Civic Association with a published charter. It may run a hall, a market, a volunteer brigade, a postal agency and similar volunteer services. It is not a council, a shire or a local government area, and it may not make rules binding on a non-consenting person.
>
> **3. Local service charges.** A Civic Association may propose a disclosed charge for disclosed local services. A charge is authorised only by an Assembly decision recording rate, purpose and duration, plus individual consent of each member charged. A charge is never a rate on land and never attaches to a parcel, title or dwelling.
>
> **4. Simulated public services.** The Assembly may constitute a Civic Service (Simulation) and fictional settlement panels for the Repository Town game. These exist only as simulation output. They have no power of arrest, detention, search, seizure, entry or identification over any real person or place; they may not fine, imprison or seize in the real world; and they refer any real incident to NSW Police and other real authorities.
>
> **5. Non-derogation.** Nothing in this Article creates territorial sovereignty, property title, exclusive jurisdiction, a right of entry, a recognised nationality, a passport, an exemption from planning law, or immunity from legal proceedings (confirms Articles IX.1, X.1, X.2).
>
> **6. Accountability.** Every charter, charge decision and ledger is public. Every simulated decision is appealable to the Ethics and Appeals Panel and has no real effect.

### 3. Article VII.3 is amended to add one sentence

> **3.** … Decisions are internal membership decisions only: the panel is **not** a court of Australian law and has no power to imprison, fine under public law or seize property. **A panel constituted under Article XIII.4 is likewise fictional, and may not fine, imprison or seize in the real world.**

### 4. Article XI.3 is amended to add one sentence

> **3.** The amendment record must include the vote result, effective date and version history. **Every amendment must state, in its own text, which real law or real authority it does not alter.**

## What this amendment does not do

- It does **not** claim any land, water, building or airspace (`LEGAL_STATUS.md`, `TERRITORY_AND_RECOGNITION.md` are unchanged).
- It does **not** create real police. There is no Parristan police service outside the game, and none may be implied.
- It does **not** change Repository Town, Narromine Shire Council, Dubbo Regional Council or any real institution. Those stand as they are.
- It does **not** make `kind` claims about the real world: Bluewater's "port" is a fictional river landing.
