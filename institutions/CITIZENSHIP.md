# Citizenship and Membership — Draft Policy

**Status:** Draft. Applications are **not** open until a private intake and review process is approved.

## Meaning of citizenship

“Citizenship of Parristan” will mean **voluntary membership in a micronational association**. It will not create a nationality recognised by a state, a right to travel or reside anywhere, entitlement to consular services, or exemption from ordinary obligations.

## Proposed eligibility

- Applicant voluntarily agrees to the constitution and peaceful principles.
- Applicant can understand the obligations of membership.
- Applicant provides an appropriate private contact method.
- A guardian can support a minor's non-voting participation when permitted by an adopted policy and applicable law.

Parristan will not demand unnecessary identity documents or publish member addresses.

## Process to establish

1. Ratify membership rules, eligibility, appeal rights and responsibilities.
2. Establish a private application channel, minimal data collection and deletion/withdrawal process.
3. Establish conflict-of-interest and application review procedures.
4. Open enrolment and issue confirmations labelled as **micronational membership**, not state-issued identity documents.
5. Publish aggregate membership totals only when accurate and safe to disclose.

## Rights proposed

Participation, access to published proceedings, ability to propose motions, fair treatment, privacy and freedom to resign.

## Responsibilities proposed

Respect lawful conduct, privacy, democratic decisions and the dignity of other members. Members are **not** required to make financial contributions or represent the nation publicly.

## Current register

No total is published until an actual, consent-based register is established. A website visit, GitHub star or repository contribution does not create citizenship.
