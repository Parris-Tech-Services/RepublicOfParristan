# Diplomacy and External Relations Policy

**Draft — no diplomatic relationships currently asserted.**

## Purpose

Build genuine, peaceful relationships with other micronational communities, cultural groups and interested researchers, without falsely presenting Parristan as a recognised state.

## Principles

- Accurate identification as a developing micronation.
- Respect for the laws of all countries and the property and rights of others.
- Truthful correspondence that never implies official government authority without evidence.
- No invented treaties, ambassadors, embassies, consulates or foreign recognition.
- No offers of passports, diplomatic immunity, banking or immigration benefits.

## Proposed correspondence procedure

1. The Assembly approves external representation or delegates limited correspondence to an accountable officer.
2. A letter identifies the sender and Parristan's current status honestly.
3. Proposals are entered into a correspondence register.
4. A reply is recorded with date, sender and scope; personal correspondence is not described as state recognition.
5. Any proposed agreement is reviewed for legality and authorised before signing.
