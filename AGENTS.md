# Coding agent guidance for Parristan

Read README.md, docs/REFERENCE_STACK_MATRIX.md, docs/FOUNDING_EXECUTION_CHECKLIST.md and docs/OPEN_SOURCE_DIGITAL_GOVERNMENT.md first.

Parristan is a developing micronation and civic initiative, not a recognised sovereign state. Its 19 November 2026 five-person founding assembly has a reported 5–0 internal constitutional ratification return (Gazette 2026-005), and a Chief Digital Officer appointment is recorded. Motion 4 confirmed the published Constitution v0.1. The original founding notice delivery time remains unrecorded. The first President, Joshua Parris, was confirmed 5–0 on 19 November 2026 (Gazette 2026-013, organiser-reported). There are no formally adopted symbols, live citizenship service or sovereign recognition. Never convert proposals or an AI-generated sample into a claim of legal authority or a completed government service. Publishing a commit is not ratification.

The French National Gendarmerie and Schleswig-Holstein are technology reference examples, not endorsers. Cite official evidence, distinguish completed deployment from ongoing migration, and never claim access to their undisclosed internal configuration.

Protect privacy. Do not commit API keys, secrets, personal addresses, contact lists, ID documents, real citizenship applications, unconsented membership data or sensitive accounts to this public repository. The organiser-supplied Operational Pack v1 includes creative mock passports, offices and game lore; those are proposals, not public records of granted status.

For each change: inspect existing files, implement a small working increment, run appropriate tests, review the diff, and report evidence. Run python scripts/validate_gazette.py (or py -3 scripts/validate_gazette.py on Windows) for site/doc changes. Do not claim live deployment until the live URL is actually checked. Preserve Gazette chronology and keep civic status claims accurate.

Prioritise accessible, open-source, open-format software: static Gazette and decision register, exportable Markdown/JSON records, document templates, and optional demo software for a future private membership workflow. Treat proposed election and citizenship systems as simulations until approved and securely operated. Use a branch and PR for substantive code, with tests and rollback notes.
