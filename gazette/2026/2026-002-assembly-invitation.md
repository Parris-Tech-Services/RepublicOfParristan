## Proposal for a first constituent assembly

The founding initiative proposes assembling a group of real, consenting participants to deliberate on Parristan's purposes, founding declaration, draft constitution and a democratic ratification process.

Suggested founding rules include independent participation, a verifiable electorate, advance notice of at least 14 days, and a properly recorded vote. These are proposals requiring agreement **before** the meeting.

**No meeting date, attendees, quorum, ballot or constitutional result has yet been verified.** This notice is a planning document and is not a summons, official law or assertion of governmental power.

See [Constituent Assembly Plan](../../institutions/FOUNDING_ASSEMBLY.md) and [Ratification Procedure](../../institutions/RATIFICATION_PROCEDURE.md).

Personal details must not be posted on public consultation threads; any actual meeting invitations require a separately approved private contact mechanism.
