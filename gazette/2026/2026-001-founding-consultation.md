## Public notice of the founding initiative

Notice is given that the **Republic of Parristan** is being established as a real, voluntary micronational civic community. The founding initiative has published a proposed declaration, draft constitution and state-building roadmap for public consideration.

The republic's founding is **a civic proposal**, not the assumption of legal sovereignty. Its constitution has **not been ratified**, no President has been elected and no land or diplomatic recognition is claimed.

## Consultation

Suggestions about democratic government, civic responsibilities, constitutional safeguards and participation are invited through [GitHub issue #1](https://github.com/Parris-Tech-Services/RepublicOfParristan/issues/1).

No ballot date or closing date has been set. Comments do not constitute votes or membership applications.

For the full constitutional proposal, see [Draft Constitution](../../institutions/CONSTITUTION_DRAFT.md).
