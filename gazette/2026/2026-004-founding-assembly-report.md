# Gazette 2026-004 — First Founding Assembly Report

**Issued:** 19 November 2026
**Event:** Thursday 19 November 2026, 3:30 pm AEDT (Sydney time)
**Classification:** Published organiser's meeting report; **ratification return awaiting exact ballot particulars**.

The founding organiser reports that the Republic of Parristan's **five founding members attended its first founding assembly** at 3:30 pm on Thursday 19 November 2026.

The organiser further reports that members confirmed consent, agreed meeting and voting procedures, considered the agenda and constitutional draft, recorded the voting process and addressed arrangements for the first election.

**This notice does not publish a YES/NO/ABSTAIN count, the adopted text's identifier, an effective date or a formally verified constitutional result**, because those particulars have not been supplied to the public record. There is also an unresolved question about the draft's proposed 14-day notice period, given that published draft documents are dated the same day as the meeting. A meeting occurring is not, by itself, proof that a constitution was adopted under valid procedures.

An evidence-based ratification return and official decision-register entry can follow when contemporaneous records are transcribed. No President, national sovereignty, Australian public-law authority or international recognition is asserted.

## Update — 19 November 2026, 3:44 pm AEDT

The founding organiser further reports that **all five founders voted YES (5–0)** on the constitution, that it takes effect on **19 November 2026**, and that the draft's notice rule is to be changed to **1 hour**. The original text of this notice is preserved above. The exact text voted on, the wording and timing of the notice rule, and reviewed minutes are still being recorded, so this is an organiser's report and not an independently verified result.
