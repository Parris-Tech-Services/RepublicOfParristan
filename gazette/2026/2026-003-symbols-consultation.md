## Invitation to consider national symbols

Original draft designs for a flag and civic coat of arms, plus an anthem lyric proposal, are offered for consultation. These are intended to give Parristan a distinctive, constructive identity while the founding constitutional process continues.

- [Proposed Flag and Arms](../../institutions/NATIONAL_SYMBOLS.md)
- [Anthem lyric proposal](../../institutions/ANTHEM_PROPOSAL.md)
- [Public feedback thread](https://github.com/Parris-Tech-Services/RepublicOfParristan/issues/2)

**No flag, coat of arms, motto or anthem has been officially adopted.** Official designation would follow a genuine constitutional decision by an authorised assembly.

No published artwork implies legal sovereignty, a diplomatic seal or official recognition.
