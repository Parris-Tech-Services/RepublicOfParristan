# Gazette No. 2026-005 — Founding Assembly Ratification and Digital Appointments

**Published:** 19 November 2026  
**Meeting:** Thursday 19 November 2026, 3:30 pm AEDT  
**Record:** Organiser-supplied minutes of the five-member founding assembly; civic/association decisions, not Australian public law.

> **Subsequent confirmation:** [Gazette 2026-009](2026-009-standing-procedures.md) records Motion 4, which confirmed the published Constitution v0.1 as the adopted text. The original founding notice time remains unrecorded.

## Decisions recorded

| Resolution | Reported decision |
| --- | --- |
| Founding-assembly notice | Unanimous **5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN** to change the *draft founding-assembly notice* from 14 days to 1 hour before the ratification ballot |
| Constitutional ratification | **5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN**; Constitution declared adopted and effective **19 November 2026** |
| Digital administration | Unanimous adoption of an open-source-first strategy inspired by France's GendBuntu and Schleswig-Holstein |
| CDO / OSPO | Glenn appointed Chief Digital Officer by unanimous consent; accepted; OSPO charter activated |

All five founding members attended, according to the minutes. The national constitution is **reported ratified as the voluntary association's internal governing document**, not proof of recognised sovereign statehood. The full meeting copy and notice time have not been provided; the consolidated published constitution reproduces the existing draft v0.1 pending textual confirmation.

The first-election dates and presidential results have **not** been supplied. The notice motion **did not amend the constitution's separate Article XI** on future constitutional amendments. The motion adopting a digital strategy and OSPO charter **did not expressly pass every section of the draft Open Source Act 2026**.

The earlier [Gazette No. 2026-004](2026-004-founding-assembly-report.md) reported the meeting while the precise vote counts were not yet supplied. This later notice **supplements, rather than silently rewrites, that historical record**.
