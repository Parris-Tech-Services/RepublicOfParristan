# Gazette No. 2026-012 — First Presidential Election Brought Forward

**Published:** 19 November 2026
**Decision:** Written resolution in the founders' interim official channel, closed 5:51 pm AEDT, 19 November 2026
**Record:** Result supplied by the organiser; civic/association decision, not Australian public law.

## Motion 6

> That the election timetable in Motion 2 ([Gazette 2026-007](2026-007-returning-officer-and-election-timetable.md)) is replaced: nominations open immediately and close the same day, 19 November 2026; if there is one valid candidate, a YES/NO confirmation vote follows the same day; voting is by email to returningofficer@parristan.org, with each vote kept confidential by the Returning Officer and only totals published. All members agree to this method.

**Result: 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN** (closed 5:51 pm AEDT, when all five had voted).

## Effect

- Replaces the 20–27 November nomination period and 28 November – 1 December voting period.
- Voting method: confidential email ballot to the Returning Officer. Votes are not secret from the Returning Officer; all members consented to this.
- The proposed seven-day nomination period in [institutions/ELECTION_RULES.md](../../institutions/ELECTION_RULES.md) had not been adopted, so no adopted rule was set aside. The Constitution's requirement of an election within 90 days of ratification is met.
