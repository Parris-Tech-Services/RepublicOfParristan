## Text of the proposed amendment

The constitutional proposal adds the following Article XIII after existing Article XII without deleting or amending Articles I–XII.

> **Article XIII — Civic and Simulated Services**
>
> **XIII.1 — Direction of adaptation.** Parristan's internal rules shall adapt to applicable Australian law and the lawful institutions of the places in which its members reside.
>
> **XIII.2 — Separation of worlds.** Fictional territories, simulated citizens, governments, courts, enforcement services and finances shall have no real-world public-law authority or legal effect.
>
> **XIII.3 — Voluntary civic services.** Parristan may organise lawful voluntary member associations, chapters and services, subject to consent, privacy protections, approved financial controls and applicable law.
>
> **XIII.4 — Preservation of rights.** Nothing in this Article overrides existing constitutional rights, confers sovereign or police powers, authorises compulsory land charges or reduces the protections governing constitutional amendments.

## Future voting conditions

Constitution Article XI requires at least **14 days' notice** of full amendment text; quorum under adopted rules; and two-thirds of valid votes cast. The founding one-hour notice exception does not apply to future amendments. The 5–0 vote on PA-2026-001 is **not** a vote on Article XIII.

**Tentative earliest practical vote: Tuesday 8 December 2026 (AEDT) or later,** *only if the actual full-text delivery to eligible members is established on 23 November.* Notice delivery and exact start time have **not** been verified. A later delivery requires a later ballot. The Secretary must confirm valid notice before scheduling the separate amendment ballot; an early ballot cannot cure a deficient notice.

No ballot, final tally, ratification, effective date, revised Constitution or public-law authority is asserted in this Gazette notice.

**Scope:** a voluntary civic association and a fictional game simulation, not real sovereign territory, an Australian police service, statutory council or enforceable land charges.
