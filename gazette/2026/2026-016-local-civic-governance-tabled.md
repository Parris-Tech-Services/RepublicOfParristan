# Gazette No. 2026-016 — Local Civic Governance: Bill and Amendment Tabled

**Published:** 23 November 2026
**Authority:** Presidential tabling under Art V.2; publication requirement under Art XI.1
**Status:** Notice running. Nothing adopted.

The President has tabled a linked pair of instruments giving Parristan's settlements a lawful internal civic form:

1. **Constitutional Amendment 2026-01 — Local Services and Simulation Authority** ([text](../../institutions/AMENDMENT_2026_01_LOCAL_SERVICES_AUTHORITY.md)). Inserts a new Article XIII, replaces Art III.3, and adds non-derogation wording to Arts VII.3 and XI.3.
2. **Bill 2026-01 — Local Civic Governance and Simulated Public Order** ([text](../../institutions/BILL_2026_01_LOCAL_CIVIC_GOVERNANCE.md)). Implements settlement Civic Associations and, once the amendment is adopted, local service charges and a Civic Service (Simulation).

## Why

Repository Town and the real town of Narromine both have civic institutions — a council, a constabulary, a courthouse, a fire service. Parristan's twelve settlements have none: no officers, no budget, no local records. The instruments give Parristan the smallest civic form that is honest about its size (a progress association, a hall committee, a volunteer brigade, a postal agency), and they bind Parristan to adapt to the wider world rather than the reverse.

## Limits, stated in the instruments themselves

- No territory, sovereignty, title or jurisdiction is claimed; `LEGAL_STATUS.md` and `TERRITORY_AND_RECOGNITION.md` are unchanged.
- No real police are created. A Civic Service (Simulation) exists only as game content and refers real incidents to NSW Police.
- No compulsory charge may fall on a non-consenting member, and no charge is ever a rate on land.
- Australian, NSW and local government law take precedence throughout (Art X.1). Repository Town, Narromine Shire Council and Dubbo Regional Council are not altered in any way.
