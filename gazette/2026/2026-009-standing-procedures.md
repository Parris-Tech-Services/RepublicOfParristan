## Motion 4 (en bloc) — How we work

1. **Voting in the official channel:** motions stay open for 48 hours, or close early once all members have voted. A motion passes on a majority of votes cast, with at least 3 members voting.
2. **Meetings:** monthly, by call or in person, arranged by the Secretary. Any member may call an extra meeting with 24 hours' notice in the channel.
3. **Records:** the Secretary keeps minutes and decisions, published in the Gazette. No personal contact details or addresses are ever published.
4. **Admin access:** Josh and Glenn are joint admins of the channel, the GitHub organisation and any future domain, so no single person can lock members out.
5. **Constitution check:** members confirm that the Constitution v0.1 published in the repository is the text adopted on 19 November 2026.
