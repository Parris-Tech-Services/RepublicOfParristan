# Gazette No. 2026-010 — Permanent Official Email Adopted (Purelymail)

**Published:** 19 November 2026
**Decision:** Written resolution in the founders' interim official channel, 19 November 2026 (closing time not supplied)
**Record:** Result reported by the organiser ("everyone said yes"); civic/association decision, not Australian public law.

## Motion 5 — Permanent email and chat

> That the Assembly adopts Purelymail email on a Parristan domain, together with the existing Matrix room, as the permanent official email and chat system.

Presented by the Chief Digital Officer (Glenn Peterson) in response to Motion 1 ([Gazette 2026-006](2026-006-interim-official-channel.md)), ahead of the 3 December 2026 deadline. Full proposal: [operations/drafts/COMMS_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md](../../operations/drafts/COMMS_PROPOSAL_TEMPLATE.md).

**Result: all five members voted YES (reported 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN).**

## Effect

- Purelymail (about US$10 a year, account-wide) is the chosen email provider. Matrix remains the chat channel and **stays the official channel until email is confirmed working**.
- **Not yet done:** domain name not chosen or registered; no Purelymail account created; no money spent. Payment needs the Treasurer to record it, as no budget is yet adopted ([Gazette 2026-008](2026-008-interim-roles-en-bloc.md)).
- Next steps: choose and register a domain (joint admins Josh and Glenn, 2FA on), set up Purelymail and DNS, test with two members, then announce official addresses in a later notice.
