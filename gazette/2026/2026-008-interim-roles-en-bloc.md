# Gazette No. 2026-008 — Interim Office-Holders and First Steps (En Bloc)

**Published:** 19 November 2026
**Decision:** Written en bloc resolution in the founders' interim official channel (FA-2026-005), 19 November 2026
**Record:** Organiser-supplied transcript of the room; civic/association decision, not Australian public law.

## Motion 3 (en bloc) — Interim roles and first steps

1. Appoint **Jane Carbunkle** as Secretary, **Fred Carbunkle** as Treasurer and **Glenn Peterson** as Registrar until the first election.
2. Members ask the President-elect to bring a draft mission and first-year goals within 14 days of the election.
3. The Registrar keeps the member list privately, never on GitHub, and issues member numbers 1–5 to the founders.
4. Parristan holds no money and no bank account until a budget is approved.

Any member could ask for an item to be voted separately; none did.

**Result: 5 YES, 0 NO, 0 ABSTAIN.**
